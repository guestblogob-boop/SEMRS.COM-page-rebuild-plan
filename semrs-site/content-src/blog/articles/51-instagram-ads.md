Instagram ads put your business in front of people as they scroll their feed, watch Stories or browse Explore. They suit businesses with something visual to show: food, beauty, fitness, home improvement, products and events. This guide covers the ad formats, how targeting and budgets work, what creative performs and how to measure results.

## Key takeaways

- They are created in Meta's ad tools and can run on Instagram, Facebook or both.
- Choose the format by goal: photo and carousel for products, video and Stories for attention, Explore for discovery.
- Track leads and sales with the Meta pixel and UTM links, not likes.

## Instagram ad formats

@img chart

Meta lists these Instagram ad formats on its business site:

| Format | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Photo ads | One image, square, landscape or portrait | A single product or offer |
| Video ads | Video in the feed | Showing a process or result |
| Carousel ads | Several photos or videos people swipe through | Product ranges, before and after, steps |
| Stories ads | Full-screen vertical ads between Stories | Time-limited offers, events |
| Explore ads | Ads in the Explore feed | Reaching people discovering new accounts |

Reels placements are also available in Meta's ad tools. Short vertical video works well there.

## How the ads work

You set them up in Meta Ads Manager, or boost a post from the Instagram app. Each campaign needs:

1. **A goal**, such as website traffic, leads, messages or sales.
2. **An audience**, by location, age, interests or people who know your business.
3. **A budget and schedule**, set per day or for the whole campaign.
4. **Placements**, Instagram only or Instagram plus Facebook.
5. **The ad**, with your image or video, text and a call-to-action button.

Housing, employment, credit and some other categories have extra targeting limits.

## Budgets: start small and test

There is no single right budget. Start with an amount you can sustain for two to four weeks, test two or three versions of the ad and keep the winner. Our guide to setting an [advertising budget](/advertising-budget/) shows how to work back from the value of a customer.

## Creative that stops the scroll

- **Show the result in the first second:** the finished room, the plated dish, the smile.
- **Use real people and real work**, not stock photos.
- **Design for sound off:** add captions to video.
- **Keep text short** and put the offer in the first line.
- **One clear call to action**: book, call, shop or message.

Likes are not sales. Set up the Meta pixel on your website and track enquiries, bookings and purchases. Tag links with UTM codes using our free [UTM builder](/tools/utm-builder/) so visits show up in your analytics. Our free [ROAS calculator](/tools/roas-calculator/) compares revenue with spend.

## Instagram ads vs Google Ads

Google search ads reach people who are already searching; Instagram creates demand among people who are not searching yet. Many businesses use both: search for ready buyers, Instagram for awareness and retargeting. Our guide to [Google Ads for small business](/google-ads-for-small-business/) covers the search side.

## Frequently asked questions

? How much does advertising on Instagram cost?
You set your own budget, per day or per campaign. What you pay per result depends on your audience, competition and how good your ad is.

? Do I need a Facebook page to advertise on Instagram?
They run through Meta's ad tools, which use a Meta business account. You can run ads on Instagram only, or on both platforms.

? Which Instagram ad format is best?
It depends on your goal. Carousels suit product ranges, video and Reels suit attention, and Stories suit time-limited offers.

? Should I boost posts or use Ads Manager?
Boosting is quick for simple goals. Ads Manager gives more control over goals, audiences and testing.
