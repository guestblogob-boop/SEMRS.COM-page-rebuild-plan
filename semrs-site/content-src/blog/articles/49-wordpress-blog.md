A WordPress blog lets your business answer customer questions, show expertise and bring in visitors from search every month. You can add a blog to an existing WordPress site in minutes, or start a new one in an afternoon. This guide covers the setup, the settings that matter, what to write first and how to turn readers into customers.

## Key takeaways

- Use self-hosted WordPress on hosting that meets WordPress's recommended specs, under your own domain.
- Set readable permalinks, one SEO plugin and a fast, simple theme before you publish.
- Write posts that answer real customer questions and link each one to a relevant service page.

[glance: WordPress blog at a glance | Your own domain | Fast, simple theme | Answer real questions | Link to services]

## Why a business blog helps

- **Search visibility:** each helpful post can rank for questions your customers ask.
- **Trust:** people see your expertise before they contact you.
- **Sales support:** you can send a post link instead of writing the same answer again.

Google's SEO Starter Guide says creating content people find compelling and useful is likely to influence search presence more than any other single suggestion in the guide.

## Start a WordPress blog in 8 steps

@img chart

1. **Choose hosting** that supports PHP 8.3 or higher and MySQL 8.4 or MariaDB 10.11 or higher, as WordPress.org recommends. Our guide to the [best WordPress hosting](/best-wordpress-hosting/) explains what else to check.
2. **Use your own domain**, such as yourbusiness.com/blog, so the blog builds your main site's authority.
3. **Install WordPress** with your host's one-click installer, or add a Posts page to your existing site.
4. **Pick a fast, simple theme** with a readable font size and good contrast.
5. **Set permalinks** to "Post name" so addresses read /how-much-does-a-roof-cost/.
6. **Add one SEO plugin** to set titles, descriptions and a sitemap.
7. **Create categories** that match your services, not dozens of tags.
8. **Write your first five posts** before you announce the blog.

## What to write first

| Post type | Example | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Cost guide | How much does a boiler replacement cost? | People search before they buy |
| How to choose | How to choose a family dentist | Builds trust at the decision stage |
| Comparison | Repair vs replace: which is right? | Helps people decide |
| Checklist | Spring maintenance checklist | Saved and shared |
| FAQ answers | Do I need a permit for a fence? | Answers questions you hear every week |

## Writing posts that rank and convert

- **Answer the question in the first paragraph.**
- **Use clear headings** and short paragraphs.
- **Add your own photos and examples.**
- **Link to the matching service page** and to related posts.
- **End with a clear next step**, such as a quote form or phone number.
- **Update older posts** each year so prices and rules stay current.

## How often should you post?

Consistency matters more than volume. One helpful post a month, kept up for a year, usually does more than ten rushed posts in the first week. Plan topics ahead in a simple calendar.

## Frequently asked questions

? Is WordPress good for blogging?
Yes. WordPress started as blogging software and still makes it easy to write, organize and publish posts, with full control over SEO settings.

? Should my blog be on a separate domain?
Usually no. A blog at yourbusiness.com/blog helps your main site, while a separate domain splits your authority.

? How much does it cost to start a WordPress blog?
WordPress itself is free. You pay for hosting and a domain, and optionally for a premium theme or plugins.

? How long does it take for a blog to bring traffic?
New posts often take a few months to rank. Steady, helpful posts build traffic over time.
