Marketing automation software sends the right message at the right time without you doing it by hand. It can welcome new subscribers, follow up on enquiries, remind customers to book again and pass hot leads to sales. For a small business, the goal is not more email. It is fewer missed follow-ups. This guide covers what these tools do, which features matter, how pricing works and the first automations to set up.

## Key takeaways

- Start with a few simple automations: a welcome series, enquiry follow-up and review requests.
- Choose software that connects to your CRM, website forms and store, so contact data stays in one place.
- Collect clear consent, and follow email and text message rules in every automation.

## What marketing automation software does

Marketing automation software runs actions when a trigger happens. A trigger can be a form submission, a purchase, a date or a click. The action can be an email, a text, a tag, a task for your team or a change in the contact's stage.

Common uses for small businesses:

- **Welcome series** for new subscribers.
- **Lead follow-up** after a quote request or booking.
- **Abandoned cart** emails for online stores.
- **Review requests** after a job is finished.
- **Re-booking reminders** for services people repeat, such as cleaning or dental checkups.
- **Lead scoring** that flags people who visit pricing pages or open several emails.

## How pricing works

Most tools charge by the number of contacts, the number of emails sent or the number of users. Many have free plans with basic email automation; full multi-step workflows often need a higher plan. HubSpot is one example: in November 2026, its Marketing Hub had a free plan with simple email automation, a Starter plan from $20 per seat a month and a Professional plan with full workflows from $800 a month plus a one-time $3,000 onboarding fee.

The lesson: price the plan that includes the automation you actually need, at the list size you expect next year. Our guide to [email marketing platforms](/email-marketing-platforms/) compares free plans for email-first tools.

## Five automations to set up first

1. **Welcome series.** Three to five emails over two weeks: thank you, who you are, your most useful guide, a customer story and a clear offer.
2. **Enquiry follow-up.** An instant reply when someone requests a quote, then a reminder to your team if no one has called within an hour.

4. **Re-booking reminder.** A reminder when a service is due again.
5. **Win-back email.** A friendly check-in for customers who have not bought in a while.

## How to choose marketing automation software

1. **List your top three flows** and check each tool can build them on the plan you will buy.
2. **Check integrations** with your CRM, website forms, booking system and store. Our guide to the [best CRM for small business](/best-crm-for-small-business/) explains why one contact record matters.
3. **Test the workflow builder** with a free plan or trial.
4. **Check deliverability tools** such as domain authentication.
5. **Compare the price** at double your current list size.
6. **Check data export**, so you can leave with your contacts and consent records.

## Email and text message rules

Automation sends more messages, so compliance matters more. In the US, commercial email must follow the [FTC's CAN-SPAM rules](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business): honest headers and subject lines, your postal address and a working unsubscribe that is honored within 10 business days. Marketing texts are stricter. Under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, you generally need the person's prior express written consent before sending automated marketing texts. Other countries, such as Canada and the UK, require consent before you email.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is marketing automation software?
It is software that sends messages and runs tasks when a trigger happens, such as a form submission or a purchase. It helps small businesses follow up quickly and consistently without doing every step by hand.

? Is there free marketing automation software?
Yes. Several tools have free plans with basic email automation, such as a welcome email. Multi-step workflows with branches usually need a paid plan.

? What is the difference between email marketing and marketing automation?
Email marketing sends campaigns to a list. Marketing automation runs flows triggered by each person's actions, and it can include texts, tasks and CRM updates as well as email.

? Do I need a CRM for marketing automation?
It helps. A CRM keeps each contact's history in one place, so your automations can use it. Some automation tools include a simple CRM.
