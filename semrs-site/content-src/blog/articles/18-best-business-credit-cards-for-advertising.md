The best business credit cards for advertising spend pay you back on the ads you already buy, such as Google, Meta and other online ads, without high fees or interest eating the reward. There is no single best card for every business. This guide explains the card types, shows real examples of cards that reward advertising and lists what to check before you apply.

## Key takeaways

- If you pay in full each month, look for a card that gives bonus rewards on advertising purchases.
- If you need time to pay, a 0% intro APR card costs less than any reward is worth.
- Business cards lack some consumer protections, and most require a personal guarantee.

## Why put advertising on a business card

Ad platforms such as Google Ads and Meta charge a card again and again as you spend. A business card keeps those costs apart from personal spending, gives you one monthly statement and can earn rewards on money you would spend anyway. It also gives you a short grace period: when you pay the full balance by the due date, you usually pay no interest.

The risk is carrying a balance. Card interest rates are high, so interest on unpaid ad spend can cost more than the ads earn.

## The three types of business credit cards

| Card type | How it pays you | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Bonus category | Extra points or cash back in set categories, which can include advertising | Businesses that spend a lot on online ads and pay in full |
| Flat rate | The same cash back on every purchase | Simple spending across many categories |
| 0% intro APR | No interest on purchases for an opening period | Businesses that need a few months to pay for a launch |

## Examples of cards that reward advertising

These examples come from the issuers' own pages in November 2026. They show what to look for; they are not recommendations.

- **Chase Ink Business Preferred:** 3 points per $1 on the first $150,000 a year in combined purchases on shipping, travel, internet, cable and phone services, and advertising with social media sites and search engines. Annual fee $95.
- **American Express Business Gold Card:** 4 points per $1 in the two categories where you spend the most each billing cycle, from a list that includes advertising in select media. The 4X rate applies to the first $150,000 in combined purchases each calendar year. Annual fee $375.
- **Chase Ink Business Unlimited:** 1.5% cash back on every purchase, no annual fee and 0% intro APR on purchases for 12 months from account opening.

A $95 or $375 fee is only worth it if your bonus rewards beat it. On $2,000 of ad spend a month, a 3X card earns 72,000 points a year. What those points are worth depends on how you redeem them, so check the issuer's redemption values.

## How to choose the best business credit cards for ad spend

@img chart

1. **Total your monthly ad spend** across Google, Meta, LinkedIn and other platforms.
2. **Decide if you will pay in full.** If not, focus on a low rate or a 0% intro period, not rewards.
3. **Check the bonus categories.** Confirm the platforms you use count as "advertising" in the card terms.
4. **Compare the fee with the rewards** at your real spend, not at the maximum cap.
5. **Check the caps.** Bonus rates often stop after a set amount each year.
6. **Read the liability terms.** Most small business cards need a personal guarantee.

## Protections business cards do not have

The Credit CARD Act of 2009 added protections for consumer cards, such as limits on raising the rate on an existing balance. Those rules generally do not apply to cards issued for business use. A Federal Reserve report to Congress notes that business cards fall largely outside the Truth in Lending Act, apart from protections against unauthorized use and unsolicited cards. Some issuers extend parts of these protections voluntarily, so read the card agreement.

Most small business cards also require a **personal guarantee**. If the business cannot pay, you are personally responsible for the debt. Late payments can hurt your personal credit too.

## Frequently asked questions

? What are the best business credit cards for advertising?
The best card depends on your spend and whether you pay in full. Cards with bonus categories for advertising, such as the Chase Ink Business Preferred or the American Express Business Gold Card, suit businesses that spend steadily on online ads. A flat-rate card is simpler if your spending is spread out.

? Do Google Ads and Facebook ads count as advertising on credit cards?
Often they do, but each issuer defines its own categories. Check the card terms for how purchases from search engines and social media sites are coded.

? Is a 0% APR business credit card a good idea for marketing?
It can be, if you have a clear plan to repay before the intro period ends. After it ends, the regular variable rate applies to the remaining balance.

? Do business credit cards affect personal credit?
Many issuers check your personal credit when you apply, and most cards need a personal guarantee. Some issuers also report late payments to the personal credit bureaus.
