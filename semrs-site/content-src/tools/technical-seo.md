These free technical SEO tools check robots.txt rules, redirects and hreflang, common causes of lost traffic. Technical SEO makes sure search engines can find, crawl and understand your website. These free tools cover the most common problems: crawler rules that block the wrong pages, redirects that go wrong after a website change, and language versions that show to the wrong country.

[semrs_tools category="technical-seo"]

## Why technical SEO comes first

Great content cannot rank if search engines cannot reach it. A single wrong line in robots.txt can block a whole website. A website redesign without proper redirects can send years of links and visitors to error pages overnight. These problems are often invisible to the business owner because the website still looks fine in a browser, which is why they are worth checking regularly.

## What to check

- **Robots.txt** allows search engines to crawl your important pages and assets, blocks only what it should, and lists your sitemap.
- **AI crawler rules** reflect your choice about AI training and AI search visibility.
- **Redirects** use a single permanent hop from each old URL to its closest new page.
- **Preferred version:** HTTP and HTTPS, www and non-www all end on one version.
- **Old links** from your Google Business Profile, directories and social profiles still work after any URL change.

## Which technical SEO tools to use

Use the **Robots.txt Generator** when you launch a site, move from a staging server, change platform or want to set rules for AI crawlers such as GPTBot and ClaudeBot. Use the **Redirect Checker** after any URL change, when an old link seems slow, or when Search Console reports redirect errors. Use the **Robots.txt Tester** to check whether Googlebot or an AI crawler may open a specific URL on a live site, and to test changes before you upload them. Use the **Redirect Rules Generator** to turn a list of old and new URLs into .htaccess, Nginx or CSV rules, and the **Hreflang Tag Generator** when a page exists in several languages or for several countries. Together they cover the first checks a technical SEO audit would make.

## A quick technical health routine

Once a month, open your robots.txt in a browser to make sure it has not changed unexpectedly, test your homepage and two or three important old URLs in the Redirect Checker, and look at the Pages report in Google Search Console for new errors. It takes ten minutes and catches most problems before they cost traffic.

Google's [crawling and indexing guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing) covers the technical basics these tools check.

## Frequently asked questions

Q: What is the most common technical SEO mistake?
A: Leaving "Disallow: /" in robots.txt after launching a site from a staging server, which blocks the whole website from search engines.

Q: Do I need a developer to fix redirects?
A: Not always. Many content management systems and SEO plugins include a redirect manager. Complex migrations are best planned with an expert.

Q: How do I know if Google can crawl my site?
A: Use the URL Inspection tool and the Pages report in Google Search Console, and check your robots.txt with the generator's rules in mind.
