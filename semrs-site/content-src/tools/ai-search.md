More people now ask AI assistants for recommendations and answers. Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making your business easy for those systems to understand, describe accurately and cite. These free tools help with the technical side.

[semrs_tools category="ai-search"]

## How AI search finds information

AI answer engines combine what their models learned during training with information fetched from the web when a question is asked. Some show links to their sources; others summarise without them. Either way, they rely on content that is crawlable, clear and consistent. If your website is vague, contradictory or blocked, AI tools are more likely to describe your business inaccurately or not at all.

## What helps your business appear in AI answers

- **Clear, factual pages** that state who you are, what you offer, where and for whom.
- **Consistent details** across your website, Google Business Profile and directories.
- **Structured data** that spells out your business information.
- **Crawler access:** AI search and browsing bots allowed in robots.txt if you want to be cited.
- **A curated guide,** such as an llms.txt file, pointing to your most useful pages.
- **Genuine reputation:** reviews and mentions on other trustworthy websites.

## Tools for AI visibility

The **llms.txt Generator** builds a short Markdown file that summarises your website and lists your best pages, following the proposed llms.txt format. Pair it with the [Robots.txt Generator](/tools/robots-txt-generator/), which lets you allow AI search crawlers while opting out of AI training, and the [Schema Markup Generator](/tools/schema-markup-generator/) for structured business facts.

## Keeping expectations realistic

GEO is a young field. No one can guarantee that an AI assistant will mention your business, and llms.txt is a proposal rather than an official standard. The most reliable strategy is the same as for search: accurate, helpful content and a consistent, trustworthy presence online. The tools here make the technical part quick so you can focus on that.

## Frequently asked questions

Q: Is GEO different from SEO?
A: It builds on SEO. Clear content, technical health and reputation help in both. GEO adds attention to how AI tools read, summarise and cite information.

Q: Should I block AI crawlers?
A: It depends on your goals. Blocking training crawlers keeps your content out of future model training; blocking search crawlers can reduce how often AI tools cite you.

Q: Does llms.txt guarantee AI visibility?
A: No. It is a simple, low-risk addition that some tools read. Content quality and consistency matter far more.
