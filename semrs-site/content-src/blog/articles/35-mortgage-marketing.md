Mortgage marketing is how brokers and loan officers get in front of home buyers and homeowners before they choose a lender. Most borrowers start with a referral or a search, then compare a few lenders on trust, speed and clear answers. This guide covers seven ideas that work, how to measure them, and the advertising and referral rules every mortgage professional must follow.

## Key takeaways

- Referral partners, local search and helpful content bring steady leads; ads add volume when tracking is in place.
- Mortgage ads that mention terms such as payments or loan length trigger extra disclosures, including the APR.
- Paying for referrals is restricted under RESPA, so build partner relationships on value, not payments.

## 7 mortgage marketing ideas that work

@img chart

### 1. Build real estate and builder partnerships

Agents, builders and financial planners see buyers before lenders do. Offer value: fast pre-approvals, clear updates during the loan and co-hosted first-time buyer workshops. Keep every arrangement within RESPA's rules.

### 2. A Google Business Profile with reviews

Borrowers search "mortgage broker near me". A complete profile and steady reviews put you in the map results. Our guide on how to get more [Google reviews](/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/) shows how to ask within the rules.

### 3. Helpful content for first-time buyers

Answer real questions: how much you need for a down payment, how credit affects your rate, and what pre-approval means. These pages bring searchers early and build trust.

### 4. Loan program pages

Give each program its own page, such as FHA, VA, USDA, conventional, jumbo and refinancing, with who it suits and what documents are needed.

### 5. Search ads for high-intent searches

Phrases such as "FHA lender [city]" or "refinance mortgage [city]" show clear intent. Send ads to the matching program page. Our guide to [Google Ads for small business](/google-ads-for-small-business/) covers the basics.

### 6. Email for past clients and partners

Past clients refinance, move and refer friends. A monthly market email keeps you in mind. Our guide to [marketing automation software](/marketing-automation-software/) shows simple flows.

### 7. Short videos that explain the process

Explain one step per video: pre-approval, appraisal, closing costs. Short, clear videos build trust on social media and your site.

## Mortgage advertising rules

**Trigger terms and the APR.** Under Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026.24), if an ad states a rate, it must state it as an annual percentage rate (APR). If an ad mentions a trigger term, such as the down payment, the number of payments, the repayment period or a payment amount, it must also disclose the down payment, the repayment terms and the APR. Check every ad, post and landing page.

**Referral fees and kickbacks.** RESPA Section 8, implemented in 12 CFR 1024.14, bans giving or accepting anything of value for referring settlement service business on a federally related mortgage loan. Co-marketing must be paid in proportion to the real value each party gets.

**Licensing details.** Many states require your NMLS ID and company details in ads and on your website. Check your state regulator's rules.

**Fair lending.** Ads must not discourage applicants based on protected characteristics. Google also restricts targeting for credit ads in the US and Canada.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is the best mortgage marketing strategy?
Most brokers and loan officers grow fastest with referral partnerships, a strong Business Profile with reviews, helpful content for first-time buyers and well-tracked search ads.

? How do loan officers get leads?
From real estate agents and builders, past clients, local search, helpful website content, search ads and community events.

? What disclosures do mortgage ads need?
If an ad states a rate, it must state the APR. If it mentions trigger terms such as a payment amount or loan term, it must also disclose the down payment, repayment terms and APR. Many states also require your NMLS ID.

? Can mortgage brokers pay real estate agents for referrals?
No. RESPA Section 8 bans paying for referrals of settlement service business on federally related mortgage loans. Some marketing arrangements are allowed if each party pays its fair share.
