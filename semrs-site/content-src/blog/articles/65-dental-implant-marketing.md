Dental implant patients research for weeks before they book. They compare costs, financing, dentists' experience and before-and-after results, and they often visit several practices for consultations. Dental implant marketing meets them at each stage with clear information, honest results and an easy way to book a consultation. This guide covers the patient journey, seven marketing steps and the rules every practice must follow.

## Key takeaways

- Implant patients research for a long time, so give them detailed, honest pages on treatment, cost and financing.
- Show real, consented before-and-after cases and reviews that mention implants.
- Keep every claim truthful and typical, and get written authorization before using patient information.

## 7 steps for dental implant marketing

1. **Build a detailed implant page** explaining the procedure, timeline, suitability and alternatives.
2. **Publish a cost guide** with honest ranges and what changes the price.
3. **Show real cases** with written patient authorization, and describe typical results.
4. **Collect implant reviews** by asking patients after their final restoration.
5. **Run search ads** for implant searches in your area, sent to the implant page. Our guide to [Google Ads for small business](/google-ads-for-small-business/) covers setup.
6. **Offer an easy consultation booking** online and by phone.
7. **Follow up** with consultation patients by email or phone, with their consent.

## Advertising and privacy rules

**Truthful claims.** The FTC's guidance on health products says health claims need competent and reliable scientific evidence. Testimonials and before-and-after images must reflect typical results; a small "results not typical" note does not fix a misleading impression.

**Patient privacy.** In the US, using a patient's health information in marketing, including identifiable photos and stories, generally needs their written authorization under HIPAA.

**State rules.** Some dental boards restrict claims about specialties and guarantees. Check yours before you advertise.

## Frequently asked questions

? How do dentists get more implant patients?
With a detailed implant page, an honest cost guide, real consented cases, implant reviews, focused search ads and easy consultation booking, followed by consistent follow-up.

? Should dentists publish implant prices?
Honest price ranges, with what changes the cost, help patients who are researching. Check your state's advertising rules on prices and offers.

? Can dentists use before-and-after implant photos?
Yes, with the patient's written authorization, and only real, typical results.

? How long do implant patients take to decide?
Many research and compare for weeks or months. Regular, helpful follow-up keeps your practice in mind.
