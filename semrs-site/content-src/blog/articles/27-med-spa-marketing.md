Med spa marketing has two jobs: bring in new clients for treatments such as injectables, laser and skin care, and keep them coming back. Clients choose a med spa on trust, so reviews, real results and clear information matter more than discounts. This guide covers seven ways to grow, plus the advertising and privacy rules every medical spa must follow.

## Key takeaways

- Most new clients search locally and compare reviews, photos and prices, so start with your Business Profile and treatment pages.
- Every claim, before-and-after photo and testimonial must be truthful, typical and used with consent.
- Memberships, rebooking and referrals usually grow a med spa faster than constant discounts.

## 7 ways to grow a med spa

@img chart

### 1. Complete your Google Business Profile

Choose the most accurate main category, list every treatment, add real photos of your rooms and team, and keep hours current. Our [Google Business Profile guide](/google-business-profile-optimization/) covers each field.

### 2. Build one page per treatment

Clients search for "Botox in Scottsdale" or "laser hair removal near me", not "aesthetic services". Give each main treatment its own page with what it is, who it suits, what to expect, downtime, price ranges or "from" prices, and who performs it. Name the licensed provider and medical director.

### 3. Earn steady reviews

Ask happy clients for a review after their follow-up visit, with a direct link. Reply calmly and generally, without confirming anyone is a client. Never buy reviews or reward only positive ones.

### 4. Show real, typical results

Before-and-after photos sell, but only real, unedited photos of your own clients, with their written permission, should be used. Show typical results and include the number of sessions. Avoid filters and lighting changes between photos.

### 5. Run targeted ads for high-value treatments

Search ads catch people ready to book. Social ads on Instagram and Facebook build interest for newer treatments. Keep claims modest, send each ad to its treatment page and track bookings, not likes. Our guide to [Google Ads for small business](/google-ads-for-small-business/) explains the setup.

### 6. Create memberships and packages

Monthly memberships, treatment packages and rebooking reminders turn one-time clients into regulars. Use email and text reminders with clear consent. Our guide to [marketing automation software](/marketing-automation-software/) shows simple flows to set up.

### 7. Share helpful content on social media

Short videos that explain treatments, aftercare and myths build trust. Introduce your providers. Keep claims accurate in posts and captions too.

## Advertising and privacy rules

**Truthful claims.** The FTC's guidance on health products says health benefit claims need competent and reliable scientific evidence. Testimonials and before-and-after images make claims of their own, so they must reflect typical results. A small "results not typical" note does not fix a misleading ad.

**Patient privacy.** In the US, using a client's health information, including identifiable photos and stories, in marketing generally needs their written authorization under HIPAA. Get signed consent for every photo and testimonial, and store it.

**Reviews.** The FTC's Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule bans fake reviews and rewarding only positive ones. Staff reviews must disclose the relationship.

**State rules.** Many states limit who can perform and advertise injectables and laser treatments, and how. Check your state's medical board rules with your medical director.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is the best marketing for a med spa?
For most med spas, a complete Google Business Profile, strong reviews and a page for each treatment bring the most bookings for the least money. Ads and memberships add growth once those basics work.

? How do med spas get more clients?
They show up in local search, prove results with real before-and-after photos, earn reviews, and keep clients with memberships and rebooking reminders.

? Can med spas use before-and-after photos?
Yes, with the client's written permission, and only real, typical, unedited results. Check your state's rules too.

? How much should a med spa spend on marketing?
It depends on your goals and market. Work out what a new client is worth over a year, then decide what you can pay to win one, and track cost per new client by channel.
