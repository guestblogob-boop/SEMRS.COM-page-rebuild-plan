## How the ideas are grouped

Every search has an intent. Matching the right page to the right intent is one of the simplest ways to rank better and convert more visitors.

- **Questions people ask** (informational): "how much does … cost", "is … worth it". Answer these in FAQ sections and helpful articles; they are also the questions AI assistants pick up.
- **Learning and guides** (informational): "… for beginners", "… checklist". These suit blog posts and guides that build trust before someone is ready to buy.
- **Comparing options** (commercial investigation): "best …", "… reviews", "… pricing". People are choosing between providers, so comparison and pricing pages work best.
- **Ready to buy or hire** (transactional): "… services", "hire …", "… quote". These belong on your service pages, with a clear price or call to action.
- **Local searches**: "… near me", "… in [city]". Add your city to get these; use them on location pages and your Google Business Profile.

4. Check demand for the most promising ideas in Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner or Google Trends.
5. Give each important intent its own page instead of mixing them: one service page for "web design services", one guide for "web design tips", one local page for "web design in Lahore".

## Example: a dentist in Lahore

Say you run a dental clinic. Enter **dentist** as the seed keyword and **Lahore** as the city. Among the ideas you will see:

- **Questions:** "how much does dentist cost", "how to choose dentist", "what to ask before hiring dentist". Rewrite these naturally on your site, for example as an FAQ called "How much does a dental check-up cost in Lahore?".
- **Comparing options:** "best dentist", "dentist reviews", "affordable dentist". These suggest a page that explains your prices and shows genuine patient reviews.
- **Ready to book:** "book dentist", "dentist consultation", "dentist price list". Make sure your booking page and price list are easy to find from your home page.
- **Local:** "dentist in Lahore", "best dentist in Lahore", "dentist near me". Use the city in your title, headings and Google Business Profile, and add a map and your full address.

Not every idea will fit your business. Remove the ones that do not, then group the rest by page.

## Turning ideas into a page plan

A simple plan keeps each page focused and stops your own pages competing with each other:

| Searcher wants to… | Page to create | Example keyword |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learn | Blog post or guide | web design tips |
| Get an answer | FAQ section or short article | how much does web design cost |
| Compare | Pricing or comparison page | web design packages |
| Buy or hire | Service page with a clear call to action | web design services |
| Find someone nearby | Location page and Google Business Profile | web design in Lahore |

Start with the pages closest to a sale (service, pricing and location pages), then add guides that answer the questions customers ask before they contact you. Link the guides to the matching service page so visitors and search engines can follow the path.

Search volume needs data from Google or a paid database. Rather than show made-up numbers, this tool gives you well-structured ideas and shows where to check real demand for free. Your own Google Search Console data is the most accurate source for your site, because it shows the searches you already appear for.

## Tips for choosing keywords

- **Start with intent, not volume.** A smaller "hire …" or "… in [city]" search often brings more customers than a big informational one.
- **Use the words your customers use.** Check your enquiry emails, reviews and sales calls for phrasing.
- **One main topic per page.** Similar ideas such as "seo pricing" and "seo cost" usually belong on the same page.
- **Write for people.** Use the keyword naturally in the title, the first paragraph and a heading, then focus on answering the question better than other pages.
