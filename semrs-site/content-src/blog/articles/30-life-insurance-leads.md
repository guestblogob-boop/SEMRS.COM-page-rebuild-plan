Life insurance leads are people who have shown interest in a policy and agreed to be contacted. Many agents start by buying leads, then learn that leads they generate themselves are cheaper, more loyal and less likely to have spoken to five other agents. This guide compares buying and generating leads, covers seven channels that work and sets out the contact rules you must follow.

## Key takeaways

- Bought leads are fast but often shared and costly; your own leads take longer but convert better and cost less over time.
- Referrals, local search, helpful content and life-event marketing bring steady leads of your own.
- Follow the Do Not Call rules, get written consent for marketing texts and keep ads truthful.

## Buying life insurance leads vs generating your own

| | Bought leads | Your own leads |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Immediate | Weeks to months to build |
| Cost per lead | Pay for every lead | Falls over time |
| Exclusivity | Often sold to several agents | Yours alone |
| Trust | Low; the person may not know you | Higher; they found you |
| Consent records | Depend on the vendor | You control them |

If you buy, ask whether leads are exclusive or shared, how old they are, how consent was collected and whether you can return bad leads. Test a small batch before committing.

## 7 ways to generate your own life insurance leads

### 1. Ask for referrals

Happy clients and their families are your best source. Ask at policy delivery and at each review. Partner with financial planners, accountants and estate attorneys who serve the same people.

### 2. Get found in local search

Many people search "life insurance agent near me". A complete Google Business Profile with reviews puts you on the map. Our [Google Business Profile guide](/google-business-profile-optimization/) shows how.

### 3. Publish helpful content

Answer real questions: "How much life insurance do I need?" or "Term vs whole life for a young family". Helpful pages bring searchers who are already thinking about cover.

### 4. Market around life events

Marriage, a new baby, a new home and a new business all raise the need for cover. Reach these people through content, partners such as mortgage brokers and realtors, and targeted ads.

### 5. Run search ads

Search ads reach people actively looking. Keep them tight by product and area, and send them to a matching page with a quote form. Our guide to [Google Ads for small business](/google-ads-for-small-business/) covers the setup.

### 6. Use email to stay in touch

A monthly email to clients and prospects keeps you in mind for reviews, referrals and new needs. Our guide to [email marketing platforms](/email-marketing-platforms/) compares free plans.

### 7. Show up in your community

Workshops, local events and sponsorships build name recognition, which makes every other channel work better.

## Follow up fast

Leads cool quickly. Call or email within minutes of an enquiry, during allowed hours. Use a CRM to track every lead and follow-up. Our guide to the [best CRM for small business](/best-crm-for-small-business/) explains what to look for.

## Contact and advertising rules

**Do Not Call.** The FTC's Telemarketing Sales Rule requires sellers to check their calling lists against the National Do Not Call Registry at least every 31 days. Calls are allowed only between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. local time. An established business relationship exception lasts 18 months after a purchase or three months after an inquiry.

**Texts and automated calls.** Marketing texts and robocalls generally need the person's prior express written consent under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act. Keep a record of how and when each lead gave consent.

**Advertising.** State insurance departments regulate insurance ads. Name the insurer where required, avoid misleading comparisons and keep a copy of every ad.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is the best way to get life insurance leads?
Most agents do best with a mix: referrals and local search for steady, low-cost leads, plus search ads or carefully chosen bought leads for volume while their own channels grow.

? Are bought leads worth it?
They can be, if they are recent, exclusive and properly consented. Test a small batch, track how many become policies and compare the cost per policy with your own channels.

? How can I get free leads?
Referrals, a Google Business Profile, helpful content and community events cost little money. They take time and steady effort.

? Can I text leads?
Only with the person's prior express written consent for marketing texts, and you must honor opt-outs. Check your state's rules as well.
