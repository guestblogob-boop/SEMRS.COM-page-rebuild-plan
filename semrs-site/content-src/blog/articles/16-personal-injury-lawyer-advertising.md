Personal injury lawyer advertising works when it reaches people in the hours and days after an accident, when they search, call and compare firms. The firms that do well pick a few channels, answer the phone fast and track which ads sign cases. This guide covers the main channels, what they cost, how to measure them and the ethics rules your ads must follow.

## Key takeaways

- Injury clients usually search on Google and call within days, so search ads, Local Services Ads and your Business Profile come first.
- Legal clicks and leads are among the most expensive in Google Ads, so track cost per signed case, not cost per click.
- Every ad must follow your state bar's advertising rules: no false or misleading claims and no promised results.

## How injury clients choose a lawyer

Most people have never hired a lawyer before their accident. They search on a phone, often from home or a hospital, with words like "car accident lawyer near me". They look at reviews, call two or three firms and hire the one that answers, sounds calm and explains the next step.

That means your advertising has three jobs:

1. **Be visible** where people search in the first days.
2. **Earn trust quickly** with reviews, clear practice pages and real photos of your team.
3. **Convert the call** with fast answers, a friendly intake script and a clear free consultation offer.

## The main personal injury lawyer advertising channels

### Google Search ads

Search ads show your firm when someone types a specific phrase, such as "truck accident attorney Houston". You choose the keywords, the area and the hours. They are fast to start and easy to measure, but they are costly in legal. In the 2025 Google Ads benchmarks, attorneys and legal services had the highest average cost per click at $8.58, against $5.26 across all industries. The average legal cost per lead was $131.63.

Keep campaigns tight: one case type per ad group, a matching landing page and a long list of negative keywords such as "jobs", "salary" and "free legal aid". Our guide to [Google Ads cost](/google-ads-cost/) shows how to set a budget from the value of a case.

### Local Services Ads

Local Services Ads show above normal search ads with a "Google Screened" badge. You pay per lead (a call or message), not per click. Google lists personal injury lawyer services as an eligible category, and your firm must pass Google's screening first. Compare both formats in [Local Services Ads vs Google Ads](/local-services-ads-vs-google-ads/).

### Google Business Profile and reviews

Your Business Profile decides whether you appear in the map results. Fill in every field, pick "Personal injury attorney" as the main category, add office photos and ask every satisfied client for a review. Our [Google Business Profile guide](/google-business-profile-optimization/) walks through each step.

### SEO and helpful content

Search engine optimization brings calls you do not pay for per click. It takes months, but it keeps working. Build one page for each case type and city you serve, and answer real questions: "How long does a car accident claim take?" or "Who pays my medical bills?" Our guide to [law firm SEO](/seo-for-attorneys/) covers the full plan.

### TV, radio, billboards and social media

Mass media builds name recognition in a market, so callers already know your firm when they search. It costs much more and is harder to track. Use a unique phone number and web address for each campaign so you can see what it brings in. On social media, short videos that explain common questions work better than "call now" posts.

## What personal injury lawyer advertising costs

Costs depend on your market, case types and competition. A few rules of thumb help:

- **Budget from a signed case, not a click.** If one case in ten leads signs, and you can afford $1,500 to sign a case, you can pay up to $150 a lead.
- **Count every cost.** Include agency fees, intake staff and call tracking, not only ad spend.
- **Compare channels by cost per signed case.** A cheap lead that never signs is not cheap.
- **Expect a learning month.** The first weeks of any paid campaign are mostly testing.

## Bar rules every injury lawyer ad must follow

Personal injury lawyer advertising is regulated by each state's rules of professional conduct. Most states base their rules on the [ABA Model Rules](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_1_communication_concerning_a_lawyer_s_services/). Model Rule 7.1 bans false or misleading communication about a lawyer's services. Common problems include:

- **Promising results** or implying past verdicts mean a new client will win.
- **Using actors or dramatizations** without the disclaimers your state requires.
- **Calling yourself a "specialist"** without a certification your state recognizes.
- **Paying for referrals** outside the limited exceptions in your state's rules.
- **Soliciting accident victims directly** by phone or in person, which most states restrict.

Some state bars also want ads filed or kept on record. Keep a copy of every ad, landing page and script with the date it ran.

## How to measure your advertising

1. **Use call tracking** with a separate number per channel.
2. **Record the source** of every new case in your intake software.
3. **Tag links** in ads, emails and social posts. Our free [UTM builder](/tools/utm-builder/) creates them in seconds.
4. **Review each month:** leads, signed cases and cost per signed case by channel.
5. **Move budget** toward the channels that sign cases at the lowest cost.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is the best advertising for personal injury lawyers?
For most firms, Google search ads, Local Services Ads and a strong Business Profile bring the fastest calls, because people search right after an accident. SEO builds lower-cost cases over time. TV and billboards work best for firms that already have intake and tracking in place.

? How much do personal injury lawyers spend on advertising?
It varies widely by market. Legal had the highest average Google Ads cost per click in the 2025 benchmarks at $8.58, with an average cost per lead of $131.63. Set your budget from the value of a signed case and the share of leads that sign.

? Can lawyers advertise on Facebook and Instagram?
Yes, if the ads follow your state's lawyer advertising rules. Avoid testimonials or results claims your state restricts, and keep a copy of every ad.

? Is it legal for a law firm to pay for leads?
Paying for advertising, including pay-per-lead ads such as Local Services Ads, is generally allowed. Paying someone to recommend you is restricted in most states. Check your state's version of Model Rule 7.2.
