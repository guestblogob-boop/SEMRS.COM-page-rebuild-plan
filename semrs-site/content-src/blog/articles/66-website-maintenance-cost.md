Website maintenance cost is what you pay each year to keep a site online, secure, fast and up to date. Many owners budget for the build and forget the upkeep, until a plugin breaks, the site slows down or it gets hacked. This guide lists every line item, shows real example prices and helps you choose between doing it yourself and a care plan.

## Key takeaways

- Budget for the domain, hosting, updates, backups, security and small edits every year.
- WordPress itself advises keeping WordPress up to date and backing up regularly, including the database.
- A care plan bundles updates, backups and edits for a fixed monthly fee; doing it yourself costs time instead.

[glance: Website maintenance cost at a glance | Domain and hosting | Updates and backups | Security | Edits and content]

## What website maintenance cost covers

| Item | What it covers | How often |
|---|---|---|
| Domain renewal | Keeping your web address | Yearly |
| Hosting | The server that runs your site | Monthly or yearly |
| Updates | WordPress, theme and plugins | Weekly to monthly |
| Backups | Copies of files and database, kept off-site | Daily or weekly |
| Security | Firewall, malware scans, login protection | Ongoing |
| Speed checks | Images, caching, Core Web Vitals | Monthly |
| Edits and content | Prices, staff, new pages, blog posts | As needed |
| Licenses | Premium plugins or themes | Yearly |

The WordPress hardening guide advises always keeping WordPress up to date and backing up your data regularly, including your databases. It also recommends strong passwords and two-step authentication.

## Signs your site needs more maintenance

- Pages are getting slower.
- Plugins show warnings or have not been updated in months.
- No recent backup exists, or it is stored only on the same server.
- Forms stopped sending emails.
- Prices, staff or services on the site are out of date.

## How to budget each year

1. **List fixed costs:** domain, hosting, paid licenses.
2. **Choose a maintenance approach:** your time or a care plan.
3. **Add a small budget for changes:** new pages, offers and seasonal updates.
4. **Plan a redesign fund** for every few years. Our guide to [website design cost](/website-design-cost/) covers build prices.

## Frequently asked questions

? How much does website maintenance cost?
It depends on the site and who does the work. Expect yearly domain and hosting fees, plus either your own time or a care plan. Published care plans, such as SEMRS's, start at $39 a month.

? What does website maintenance include?
Updates, backups, security, speed checks, uptime monitoring and small content edits, plus domain and hosting renewals.

? How often should a WordPress site be updated?
Check for updates at least monthly, and apply security updates promptly. WordPress's own guidance is to keep the site up to date.

? Can I maintain my website myself?
Yes, if you have time to update, back up and check the site regularly. A care plan suits owners who would rather focus on their business.
