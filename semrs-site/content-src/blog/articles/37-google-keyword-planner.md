Google Keyword Planner is Google's own tool for finding keywords and estimating how often people search for them. It lives inside Google Ads, but you can use it for SEO planning too, without spending money on ads. This guide shows how to set it up, find keyword ideas, read the numbers correctly and turn them into a content plan.

## Key takeaways

- Keyword Planner is free, but you need a Google Ads account with billing details entered to use it.
- It gives keyword ideas, monthly search estimates, bid ranges and forecasts.
- Treat volumes as estimates and use bids as a sign of commercial value, not as an SEO difficulty score.

[glance: Google Keyword Planner at a glance | Free with an Ads account | Ideas and volumes | Bids show value | Plan one page per topic]

## What Google Keyword Planner does

According to Google's help page, Keyword Planner can:

- **Suggest keyword ideas** related to your business, products or website.
- **Estimate monthly searches** for each keyword.
- **Show the typical cost** for an ad to appear on a keyword.
- **Group keywords** into categories.
- **Forecast** clicks and costs for a planned budget.

Google also notes that keywords with very low search volume may not be shown or forecast.

## How to set up Keyword Planner without running ads

1. **Create a Google Ads account** with your business Google account.
2. **Complete account setup.** Google requires billing information before you can use basic features such as keyword ideas.
3. **Skip or pause any campaign** the setup creates. A paused campaign costs nothing.
4. **Open Tools, then Keyword Planner.**

If you already run ads, Keyword Planner is in the same menu.

## Two ways to use the Google Keyword Planner tool

@img chart

| Option | What you enter | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Discover new keywords | Words, a website or a page address | Related keyword ideas with volumes and bids |
| Get search volume and forecasts | Your own list of keywords | Volumes, bids and forecasts for that list |

**Discover new keywords** is best for brainstorming. Enter a few seed words, such as "boiler repair" and "boiler service", and set the location to the area you serve. Entering a competitor's page address shows the themes Google connects with it.

**Get search volume and forecasts** is best when you already have a list. Paste your keywords and download the results to a spreadsheet.

## How to read the numbers

- **Average monthly searches:** an estimate, which may be shown as a range (for example 1K–10K) in accounts with little ad spend. Use it to compare keywords, not as an exact count.
- **Three-month and year-over-year change:** shows rising or seasonal topics.
- **Competition:** how many advertisers bid on the keyword. It is about ads, not how hard the keyword is to rank for in organic search.
- **Top of page bid (low and high range):** what advertisers have paid to appear at the top. Higher bids usually mean the searcher is closer to buying.

Always set the location and language to match your customers. National numbers can mislead a local business.

## Turn Keyword Planner data into an SEO plan

1. **Group close variants** into one topic. "Boiler repair cost" and "boiler repair price" belong on one page.
2. **Check search intent** by searching the keyword yourself and looking at the top results.
3. **Pick one main keyword per page.** Our guide to [keyword research](/keyword-research/) explains how to map keywords to pages.
4. **Start with specific, lower-competition topics** you can realistically rank for.
5. **Track results** in Google Search Console, which shows the real searches your pages appear for.

## Frequently asked questions

? Is Google Keyword Planner free?
Yes. There is no charge to use it, but you need a Google Ads account with billing details entered. You do not have to run or pay for ads.

? Why does Keyword Planner show ranges instead of exact volumes?
Accounts with little or no ad spend may see search volumes as ranges. Use them to compare keywords; the relative size is what matters for planning.

? Is Keyword Planner good for SEO?
Yes, for ideas and search volumes. Its competition score is about advertisers, so check the search results to judge how hard a keyword is to rank for.

? How accurate is Google Keyword Planner?
Its numbers are estimates based on Google's data and are rounded or grouped for similar keywords. They are best used to compare options, then checked against Search Console once pages are live.
