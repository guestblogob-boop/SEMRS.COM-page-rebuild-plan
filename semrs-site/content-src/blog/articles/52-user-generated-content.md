User generated content is any photo, video, review or post that your customers create about your business. It is often more trusted than your own marketing, because it shows real people and real results. Using it well means asking permission, crediting the creator and following the rules on endorsements. This guide shows how to collect it and use it the right way.

## Key takeaways

- Always ask permission before reposting a customer's photo or video, and credit them.
- Disclose any free product, discount or payment linked to a post, as FTC rules require.
- Never reward only positive reviews or edit testimonials to change their meaning.

## Types of user generated content

- **Photos and videos** customers post of your product or work.
- **Reviews** on Google and other sites.
- **Testimonials** you collect directly.
- **Social posts** that tag or mention your business.
- **Questions and answers** from customers in comments or forums.

## How to use customer content the right way

@img chart

1. **Ask permission** in writing, even a short "Can we share this?" reply. Save the reply.
2. **Credit the creator** with their handle or name, if they agree.
3. **Disclose connections.** If you gave the person a free product, a discount or payment, say so clearly.
4. **Keep it honest.** Do not edit a post or review to change what the customer meant.
5. **Show typical results.** Exceptional results presented as normal can mislead.
6. **Respect privacy.** Avoid showing other people, addresses or health details without consent.
7. **Store records** of permissions in one place.

## What the FTC says

The FTC's guide to its Endorsement Guides explains that a connection between you and the person posting, such as payment or a free product, should be disclosed clearly when people would not expect it. Even a small incentive can need disclosure if it could affect how people weigh the post. Each new endorsement made without a disclosure could be deceptive.

The FTC also says you cannot make an incentive, such as a discount, depend on a review being positive, and endorsements must reflect the honest opinions or experiences of the person.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is user generated content?
It is content your customers create about your business, such as photos, videos, reviews and social posts.

? Do I need permission to repost customer photos?
Yes. Ask first, credit the creator and keep a record of their permission, especially before using the content in ads.

? Do I need to disclose free products given to customers who post?
Yes, if it is a connection people would not expect. The FTC says such connections should be disclosed clearly.

? Can I offer a discount for a review?
You cannot make an incentive depend on the review being positive, and Google does not allow incentives for reviews on Maps.
