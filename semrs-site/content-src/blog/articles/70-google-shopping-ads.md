Google Shopping ads show a product's photo, title, price and store name right in Google results, before the shopper clicks. For online stores they are often the most direct way to reach people who are ready to buy. This guide explains how they work, what drives the cost and how a small store can set up a first campaign.

## Key takeaways

- Shopping ads come from your product data in Google Merchant Center, not from keywords you write.
- You pay per click; clean product titles, images and prices decide most of the results.
- Free product listings exist too, so start there and add paid Shopping ads when you are ready.

## How Shopping ads work

Shopping ads are built from a product feed: a list of your products with their details. You upload it to Google Merchant Center, link Merchant Center to Google Ads and run a campaign. Google then matches your products to searches using the information in the feed.

That is why the feed matters so much. If the title doesn't say what the product is, Google can't match it to the right searches.

## What a Shopping ad is made of

@img chart

Each ad uses attributes from your feed. Google's product data specification lists what is required, such as availability, and what helps, such as the right product category or GTIN.

| Feed attribute | What it does |
|---|---|
| Title | The main text shoppers read; say what the item is |
| Image link | The photo; clear, on a plain background, no text over it |
| Price | Must match the price on your product page |
| Availability | In stock, out of stock or preorder |
| Brand and GTIN | Help Google identify the exact product |
| Product type and category | Help match the product to searches |

## Google Shopping ads cost

Shopping ads are charged per click: Google's help center says you are charged using cost-per-click (CPC), only when someone clicks. There is no fixed price. What you pay depends on your bid, the competition for that product and how well your ads perform.

- **Set a daily budget** you are comfortable testing for a few weeks.
- **Watch cost per sale,** not just cost per click.
- **Split products** into groups so best sellers get more budget.

## Start free: product listings

Google Merchant Center also offers free listings. If your products are eligible, they can show on Google at no cost alongside any paid ads. Setting up a clean feed for free listings is the first half of the work for Shopping ads, so it is a sensible place to start.

## 6 steps to your first Shopping campaign

1. **Create a Merchant Center account** and verify your website.
2. **Add your products** with a feed or a plugin such as the WooCommerce or Shopify integration.
3. **Fix feed errors** shown in Merchant Center before you spend anything.
4. **Link Merchant Center to Google Ads** and set up conversion tracking.
5. **Start one campaign** with a modest daily budget and your best sellers.
6. **Review weekly:** search terms, cost per sale and products that spend without selling.

## Hire an agency or do it yourself?

A small store with a few dozen products can run Shopping ads itself, as long as someone checks the feed and results every week. An agency helps when you have hundreds of products, many feed errors or rising costs. Ask any agency who owns the accounts, how they report sales and what they charge. ## Frequently asked questions

? What are Google Shopping ads?
Product ads that show a photo, title, price and store name in Google results. They come from your product data in Merchant Center.

? How much do Shopping ads cost?
You pay per click, and the price depends on bids, competition and ad quality. You control spend with a daily budget.

? Do I need a website to run Shopping ads?
Yes. Shoppers click through to your product page, and the price and availability there must match your feed.

? Are Google Shopping listings free?
Free listings are available for eligible products. Paid Shopping ads give you more placements and control.
