A digital marketing strategy is a written plan for how your business will reach customers online, which channels you will use, what you will spend and how you will know it works. Without one, marketing becomes a string of random posts and ads. This guide builds a simple plan in seven steps, with a 90-day plan you can start this month.

## Key takeaways

- Start with one clear audience and one offer, then pick two or three channels you can do well.
- Set goals you can measure, such as leads or bookings each month, and track them from day one.
- Review the plan every month and update it at least once a year.

## Build your digital marketing strategy in 7 steps

@img chart

The U.S. Small Business Administration's guide to marketing plans covers the same core parts: target market, competitive advantage, goals, an action plan, a budget and regular measurement.

### 1. Define your target customer

Describe your best customers: who they are, where they are, what problem they need solved and how they search for help. Talk to a few real customers and read your reviews. Their words become your keywords and headlines.

### 2. Clarify your offer and advantage

Why should someone choose you? Faster service, a specialist skill, clear prices, local knowledge or better guarantees. Put it in one sentence and use it everywhere.

### 3. Set measurable goals

| Weak goal | Measurable goal |
|---|---|
| Get more customers | 20 qualified leads a month by March |
| Be on social media | Two posts a week and 30 site visits a month from social |
| Rank on Google | Top three map results for "plumber [town]" within six months |

### 4. Choose your channels

Pick two or three channels that fit how your customers buy:

- **Local businesses:** Google Business Profile, reviews, local SEO and search ads.
- **Online stores:** search, shopping ads, email and social.
- **Professional services:** SEO content, LinkedIn, referrals and email.

Our guide to [SEO for small business](/seo-for-small-business/) covers search, and our guide to [Google Ads for small business](/google-ads-for-small-business/) covers paid search.

### 5. Set a budget

Decide what you can spend each month on tools, content, ads and help. Fund fewer channels properly rather than many channels thinly. Our guide to [digital marketing cost](/how-much-does-digital-marketing-cost/) shows typical prices and pricing models.

### 6. Plan your content

Make a simple calendar: one helpful article or page a month, two social posts a week, one email a month. Each piece should answer a customer question and lead to a next step. Our guide to [keyword research](/keyword-research/) helps you choose topics.

### 7. Measure and adjust

Set up tracking before you start: calls, forms, bookings and sales. Check results every month. Keep what brings leads at a good cost and cut what does not. Our free [UTM builder](/tools/utm-builder/) and [ROAS calculator](/tools/roas-calculator/) help.

## A 90-day digital marketing plan

| Month | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations | Tracking, Business Profile, review requests, fix key pages |
| 2 | Content and search | Service pages, one helpful article, local SEO basics |
| 3 | Growth | Small ad test, email to past customers, review results |

## Common planning mistakes

- **Trying every channel at once.**
- **No tracking**, so nobody knows what works.
- **Copying big brands** with budgets you do not have.
- **Changing direction every week.** Most channels need months.
- **Forgetting existing customers**, who are cheaper to sell to than new ones.

## Do it yourself or hire an agency?

Many owners write the strategy themselves and hire help for the parts that need skill or time, such as SEO, ads or content. If you hire, keep your accounts and data in your own name and ask for monthly reports on leads, not just activity.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is a digital marketing strategy?
It is a written plan for how a business reaches customers online: who it targets, what it offers, which channels it uses, its budget and how it measures results.

? How do I create a digital marketing strategy for a small business?
Define your customer and offer, set measurable goals, choose two or three channels, set a budget, plan content and measure results every month.

? What makes a good marketing plan?
A good strategy is focused, measurable and realistic. It picks a few channels that match how your customers buy and is reviewed regularly.

? How often should I update my marketing strategy?
Check results monthly and update the full plan at least once a year, or when your services, market or budget change.
