A content strategy decides what you publish, for whom, why and how you will know it works. Without one, businesses post randomly and wonder why nothing happens. With one, every article, video and email answers a real customer question and moves people closer to buying. This guide builds a simple plan in seven steps.

## Key takeaways

- Start from your customers' real questions, not from what you want to say.
- Choose three or four content pillars that match your services, and publish on a steady schedule.
- Measure enquiries and sales from content, then update what works.

[glance: Content strategy at a glance | Customer questions | Three or four pillars | Steady calendar | Measure and update]

## What Google rewards

Google's guidance on helpful content calls for people-first content: content made mainly for people, not to manipulate search rankings. It suggests asking whether your content provides original information, research or analysis, and why you made it. A good plan builds that in from the start.

## Build a content strategy in 7 steps

@img chart

### 1. Set goals

Pick one or two goals, such as "10 quote requests a month from the blog" or "grow the email list to 1,000".

### 2. Collect customer questions

List questions from calls, emails, reviews and sales conversations. These become your topics. Our guide to [keyword research](/keyword-research/) shows how to check which ones people search.

### 3. Choose content pillars

Pillars are the three or four big themes you will cover. For a roofing company: repairs, replacement, materials and costs. Every piece should fit a pillar.

### 4. Pick formats

| Format | Best for |
|---|---|
| Blog guides | Search traffic and detailed answers |
| Short videos | Social reach and showing your work |
| Emails | Staying in touch with leads and customers |
| Case studies | Proof for people close to buying |
| FAQs | Quick answers on service pages |

### 5. Plan a calendar

Plan a month at a time: for example, two guides, eight social posts and one email. Consistency beats bursts.

### 6. Distribute every piece

Share each guide in your email, on social media and in sales replies. Turn one guide into several social posts.

### 7. Measure and update

Track visits, enquiries and sales from content. Update guides that rank but convert poorly, and refresh facts and prices every year.

## Content strategy for small teams

If time is short:

- **One helpful guide a month** is enough to build momentum.
- **Reuse:** one guide becomes posts, an email and a short video.
- **Batch:** write and schedule a month in one sitting.
- **Use AI for drafts**, with a person checking facts. Our guide to [ChatGPT for marketing](/chatgpt-for-marketing/) explains a safe workflow.

## Common mistakes

- Writing about what you want to sell rather than what customers ask.
- Chasing every trend instead of building your pillars.
- Publishing without distributing.
- Never measuring enquiries from content.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is a content strategy?
It is a plan for what content you create, for which audience, why, where you share it and how you measure results.

? How often should a small business publish content?
Choose a pace you can keep. One helpful guide a month, kept up for a year, usually beats a burst of posts that stops.

? What are content pillars?
They are the three or four main themes your content covers, usually matching your main services or products.

? How do I measure content success?
Track visits, time on page, enquiries, sign-ups and sales that start from content, and compare them with the time and money spent.
