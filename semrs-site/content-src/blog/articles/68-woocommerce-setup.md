WooCommerce setup takes a WordPress site and turns it into an online store. WooCommerce is a free, open-source plugin, so the main costs are hosting, a domain, any paid extensions and payment fees. This guide walks through the eight steps a small store needs, from choosing hosting to a safe test order.

## Key takeaways

- WooCommerce is free; budget for hosting, a domain, payment fees and any paid extensions.
- Set up payments, shipping zones and taxes before you add many products.
- Place a real test order on your own store before you launch.

## The 8-step WooCommerce setup

@img chart

1. **Install the plugin.** In WordPress, go to Plugins → Add New, search for WooCommerce and activate it.
2. **Run the setup wizard.** Enter your store address, industry and product types.
3. **Add your products.** Write a clear title and description, set the price and stock, and add real photos.
4. **Set up payments.** Connect a payment provider and switch on the methods your customers use.
5. **Create shipping zones.** Add each region you ship to and its rates, plus free shipping rules if you offer them.
6. **Configure taxes.** Turn on tax calculation and set rates for where you must collect tax. Ask an accountant if you are unsure.
7. **Add store pages and policies.** Check the shop, cart, checkout and account pages, then add returns, privacy and terms pages.
8. **Place a test order.** Buy one product yourself, check the emails, then refund it.

## WooCommerce hosting: shared or managed?

| Option | Good for | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Shared hosting | New stores with few orders | Slows down when traffic grows |
| Managed WooCommerce hosting | Busy stores that need speed and support | Higher monthly cost |
| VPS or cloud hosting | Large stores with a developer | You manage more yourself |

Whatever you choose, check backups, SSL, staging and support hours. Managed WooCommerce hosting often includes caching and updates, which saves time for busy owners.

## After launch: keep the store healthy

- Update WordPress, WooCommerce and extensions regularly, after a backup.
- Watch stock, failed payments and abandoned carts every week.
- Add helpful content and category descriptions for search. Our [ecommerce SEO](/ecommerce-seo/) guide covers the details.
- Keep only the extensions you use; each one adds weight.

## Frequently asked questions

? Is WooCommerce free?
Yes. The core plugin is free and open source. You pay for hosting, your domain, payment fees and any paid extensions or themes.

? How long does WooCommerce setup take?
A small store with a few products can be ready in a day or two. Writing good product pages and photos usually takes the most time.

? Do I need special hosting for WooCommerce?
Any good WordPress host can run a small store. Busy stores benefit from managed WooCommerce hosting with caching and support.

? Can I sell services or digital products with WooCommerce?
Yes. WooCommerce supports physical, digital and virtual products, and extensions add bookings and subscriptions.
