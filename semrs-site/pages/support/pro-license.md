This guide shows you how to install SEMRS Pro and activate your SEMRS Pro license key. It takes about two minutes. SEMRS Pro is an add-on. It needs the free SEMRS Core plugin. The Pro blocks also need the free SEMRS Blocks plugin.

## Activate your license

![SEMRS Pro license steps: buy, download, upload, paste the key, done](pro-license-steps)

1. Download `semrs-pro.zip` from your purchase email or your account.
2. Go to **Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin**.
3. Choose the zip file. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
4. Go to **SEMRS → License**. Paste your key and click **Activate license**.

The status changes to **Active**. The Pro features unlock at once.

## What Pro unlocks

| Area | What you get |
|---|---|
| Blocks | Slider, animated counter, 3D viewer, scroll animations, display conditions |
| Marketing | Popups that open after a delay, on scroll or when someone is about to leave |
| SEO | Redirects, 404 log, local SEO, instant indexing, bulk SEO editor |
| Speed | Delay chat and tracking scripts, cache preload, CDN |
| Ads | More ad places, targeting, A/B tests, click protection |
| Theme | Dark mode switch, mega menu, white label |
| Languages | Language switcher and linked translations |

## Add a dark mode switch

1. Go to **Customize → SEMRS Theme → Header builder**.
2. Under **Dark mode switch**, choose how it starts: light, or the visitor's own setting.
3. Drag the **Dark / light switch** into your header.
4. Click **Publish**.

The visitor's choice is saved in their own browser.

## Add more languages

1. Go to **SEMRS → Languages**. Add one language per line, for example `en | English`. The first line is your main language.
2. Add the **Language switcher** to your header.
3. To translate a page, make a new page with the translated text. In the sidebar, open **Language & translations**. Pick its language and the original page. Save.

Google then shows the right language to each visitor. If a page has no translation yet, the switcher can link to Google Translate instead.

## Move your key to another site

![Move a SEMRS Pro license key: deactivate on the old site, then activate on the new one](pro-license-move)

1. On the old site, go to **SEMRS → License** and click **Deactivate license**.
2. On the new site, paste the key and click **Activate license**.

**Good to know:** test sites do not use up a slot. This includes addresses with localhost, .local, .test, staging. or dev.

## Updates

With an active key, WordPress tells you when an update is ready. Update SEMRS Pro in one click, just like any other plugin.

## When your license ends

Your Pro features keep working. Renew to keep getting updates, fixes and support.

## What data is sent

Only your license key, your site address and the plugin version go to semrs.com. No content and no visitor data is sent.
