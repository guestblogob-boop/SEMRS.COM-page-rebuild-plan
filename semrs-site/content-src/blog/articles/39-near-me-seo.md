Near me SEO is about showing up when someone searches "plumber near me", "coffee near me" or "dentist open now". These searches come from people who want to act soon, often within the hour. You cannot add "near me" to your pages to rank; Google works out who is near from the searcher's location. This guide explains how Google chooses businesses for nearby searches and seven steps to appear more often.

## Key takeaways

- Google ranks local results mainly by relevance, distance and prominence; you cannot pay for a better local ranking.
- A complete, accurate Google Business Profile with steady reviews is the biggest lever.
- Do not stuff "near me" into pages or your business name; use your real service areas instead.

## How Google chooses businesses for near me searches

Google's help page on local ranking says results are based mainly on three things:

- **Relevance:** how well your business matches the search, based on your categories, services and information.
- **Distance:** how far your business is from the searcher or the place in the search.
- **Prominence:** how well known your business is, including reviews, ratings, links and mentions across the web.

Google also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Ads can appear in or above map results, but they are labeled as ads.

## 7 steps to improve near me SEO

@img chart

### 1. Complete your Google Business Profile

Choose the most accurate main category, add every service, set correct hours and holiday hours, and upload real photos. Our [Google Business Profile guide](/google-business-profile-optimization/) covers every field.

### 2. Keep your hours accurate

"Open now" is part of many nearby searches. If your hours are wrong, you lose those searches and frustrate customers.

### 3. Earn steady reviews

Ask every happy customer for a review with a direct link, and reply to all reviews. Our guide on how to get more [Google reviews](/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/) shows how to do it within Google's rules.

### 4. Build pages for the areas you really serve

Create a page for each main town or district you cover, with real local details: jobs you have done there, travel times and local rules. Avoid copying the same page with only the town name changed.

### 5. Keep your name, address and phone consistent

List your business with the same details on your website, directories and social profiles. Mixed details confuse search engines and customers.

### 6. Make your site fast on phones

Most nearby searches happen on phones. Show your phone number and a call button at the top of every page, and keep pages light.

### 7. Add local business schema

Structured data tells search engines your address, hours and services. Our free [local business schema generator](/tools/local-business-schema-generator/) creates it.

## Near me SEO mistakes to avoid

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| Adding "near me" to your business name | Against Google's guidelines; can get your profile suspended |
| Stuffing "near me" into page text | Reads badly and does not help Google work out your location |
| Fake addresses or virtual offices | Breaks the rules and can remove you from Maps |
| Wrong hours | Loses "open now" searches |
| Ignoring reviews | Weakens prominence and trust |

## Service area businesses

If you visit customers rather than having them visit you, such as plumbers, cleaners or mobile mechanics, set up a service area profile and hide your home address. Your distance is then judged from your business location, so focus on relevance and prominence for the wider area.

## Frequently asked questions

? How do I rank for "near me" searches?
Complete your Google Business Profile, keep hours accurate, earn reviews, build pages for the areas you really serve and keep your details consistent online. Google works out who is near from the searcher's location.

? Should I put "near me" on my website?
No. Google does not need it to understand location, and stuffing it into pages or your business name can hurt you. Use your real town and service area names instead.

? Can I pay Google to show up in near me results?
You cannot pay for a better local ranking. You can run ads that appear in or above the map results, labeled as ads.

? Why does a competitor farther away rank above me?
Distance is only one factor. A competitor with a more relevant profile, more reviews or a stronger online reputation can outrank a closer business.
