A Google Ads agency plans, builds and manages your paid search campaigns so you get more leads or sales for your budget. A good one sets up tracking, cuts wasted spend and reports results in plain words. A poor one can lock you into contracts, hide data and judge success by clicks. This guide explains what agencies do, how they charge and the nine questions to ask before you sign.

## Key takeaways

- Insist that the ad account is in your name, with your card paying Google directly.
- Ask how the agency tracks leads and sales; reports should show cost per lead, not just clicks.
- Compare fees in dollars at your real budget, and avoid long lock-in contracts.

## What a Google Ads agency does

- **Sets up tracking** for calls, forms, bookings and sales before spending.
- **Researches keywords** and builds campaigns around services and areas.
- **Writes ads** and recommends landing page changes.
- **Manages bids and budgets**, adding negative keywords to cut waste.
- **Tests** ads, offers and pages over time.
- **Reports** results and next steps each month.

## When to hire an agency, and when not to

An agency makes sense when ads are a main source of customers, your budget is large enough that waste hurts, or you do not have the time to check campaigns every week. If you spend a small amount and have time to learn, managing your own account can work. Our [Google Ads for small business guide](/google-ads-for-small-business/) explains the basics.

## How Google Ads agencies charge

| Pricing model | How it works | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Percentage of ad spend | A share of what you spend on Google | An incentive to raise your budget |
| Flat monthly fee | A fixed fee for agreed work | Scope limits as you grow |
| Hourly | Pay for time used | Unclear total cost |
| Performance-based | Pay per lead or sale | Lead quality and who defines a "lead" |

Some agencies also charge a setup fee. Always ask for the fee in dollars at your real budget. For example, SEMRS charges 15% of each platform's ad spend, with a minimum of $30 a month per platform, and your card pays Google directly. ## What Google Partner status means

The Google Partners badge shows that an agency meets Google's current requirements. According to Google's help page, these include an optimization score of at least 70% on its manager account, at least $10,000 in ad spend across managed accounts over 90 days and certified staff. It is a useful sign of activity and training, but it does not prove results for a business like yours. Ask for the agency's approach and references too.

## 9 questions to ask a Google Ads agency

@img chart

1. **Who owns the ad account?** It should be in your name, with admin access for you.
2. **How will you track leads and sales?** Expect call, form and booking tracking before launch.
3. **What do you charge, in dollars, at my budget?** Get setup and monthly fees in writing.
4. **How long is the contract?** Monthly terms with notice are fairer than a one-year lock-in.
5. **Who will manage my account?** Ask about their experience and how many accounts they run.
6. **What will you do in the first 90 days?** A clear plan beats vague promises.
7. **What will the monthly report show?** Look for cost per lead, lead quality and next steps.
8. **How do you handle Google's recommendations?** Good agencies review each one, not accept all.
9. **Can you share examples from similar businesses?** Ask for real, checkable examples.

## Red flags to avoid

- **Guaranteed rankings or results.** No one controls Google's auction.
- **The agency owns your account.** If you leave, you may lose your data and history.
- **Hidden ad spend.** You should see exactly what Google charged.
- **Reports full of clicks and impressions** but no leads or sales.
- **Long contracts with exit fees** before the agency has proven itself.

## How to measure your agency

Check monthly: leads, cost per lead, leads that became customers and revenue where you can track it. Our free [ROAS calculator](/tools/roas-calculator/) shows the return on your ad spend. Give a new agency about 90 days to test and improve, then judge it on cost per lead and the quality of those leads.

## Frequently asked questions

? How much does a Google Ads agency cost?
Fees vary by model. Many agencies charge a percentage of ad spend, often with a monthly minimum, while others charge a flat fee. Ask for the total monthly cost in dollars at your budget, plus any setup fee.

? Is a Google Partner agency better?
Partner status shows the agency meets Google's spend, performance and certification requirements. It is a good sign, but it does not guarantee results for your business. Judge the agency's plan, reporting and references as well.

? Should I own my Google Ads account?
Yes. The account should be in your business's name, with your payment method, so you keep your data and history if you change agencies.

? How long before a Google Ads agency shows results?
Ads can bring leads in the first weeks, but the first month is mostly testing. Expect about 90 days to judge an agency on cost per lead.
