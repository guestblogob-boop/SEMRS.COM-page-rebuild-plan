Listing marketing is everything an agent does to get a home seen by the right buyers: photos, video, the description, social posts, emails, signs and open houses. Strong listing marketing helps a home sell and wins the next seller, who sees how you present properties. This guide covers nine ideas and the fair housing rules every listing ad must follow.

## Key takeaways

- Professional photos and an honest, specific description do most of the work.
- Promote each listing across portals, social media, email and your own website.
- Describe the property and area, never the kind of buyer you want; fair housing rules apply to every ad.

## 9 listing marketing ideas

@img chart

1. **Professional photos** in good light, with wide but honest angles.
2. **A video walkthrough** or short vertical clips for social media.
3. **A floor plan** so buyers understand the layout.
4. **A specific description:** room sizes, recent updates, natural light and what is nearby.
5. **A property page on your website** with all photos, details and a contact form.
6. **Social posts and ads** for the listing, using the housing ad category where required.
7. **Email to your buyer list** and to agents who sell in the area.
8. **Signs and flyers** with a QR code to the property page.
9. **Open houses** promoted a week ahead, with follow-up to every visitor.

## Writing a listing description

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Stunning home in a great area | Three-bedroom home a five-minute walk from Riverside Park |
| Perfect for young families | Fenced backyard and a fourth room that works as an office |
| Updated kitchen | Kitchen updated in 2025 with quartz counters and new appliances |

Be specific and accurate, and describe features rather than people.

## Fair housing rules for listing ads

The fair housing regulation on discriminatory advertisements (24 CFR 100.75) bans ads that show a preference, limitation or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status or national origin. That includes words, images and even where ads are placed.

Online ad platforms also limit targeting for housing ads. Google's personalized advertising policy says housing ads in the US and Canada cannot target by age, gender, parental status, marital status or ZIP code. Every listing is also marketing for you. Share before-and-after staging, just-listed and just-sold posts, and a short story of how the home sold. Future sellers notice agents who present homes well.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is listing marketing?
It is the set of activities that promote a home for sale: photos, video, descriptions, property pages, social media, email, signs and open houses.

? What makes a good listing description?
Specific, accurate details about the home and area: room sizes, updates, light and nearby amenities. Avoid describing the kind of buyer you want.

? Can I target real estate ads by age or ZIP code?
On Google in the US and Canada, housing ads cannot target by age, gender, parental status, marital status or ZIP code. Meta also requires a special ad category for housing.

? Should every listing have its own web page?
Yes, when you can. A property page on your site gives buyers one place for all details and brings enquiries to you.
