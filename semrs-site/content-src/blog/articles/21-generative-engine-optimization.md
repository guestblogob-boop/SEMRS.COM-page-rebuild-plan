Generative engine optimization, or GEO, means making your content easy for AI answer engines to find, understand and cite. Tools such as Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT search and Perplexity write an answer and link a handful of sources. GEO is about being one of those sources. This guide explains how it works, what Google itself says and the practical steps that help a small business get cited.

## Key takeaways

- GEO builds on SEO: AI answers mostly draw on pages that are already crawlable, indexed and trusted.
- Clear answers, facts with sources and well-structured pages are easier for AI tools to quote.
- Google says there are no extra requirements or special files needed to appear in AI Overviews.

## What generative engine optimization means

Traditional search shows a list of links. Generative engines write an answer first and cite sources inside or beside it. The term "generative engine optimization" comes from a 2023 research paper by Aggarwal and colleagues, later accepted at the KDD 2024 conference. In their tests, methods such as adding citations, quotations and statistics improved a source's visibility in AI answers by up to 40%, with results varying by topic.

For a business, the goal is simple: when someone asks an AI tool a question in your field, your page should be the clearest, most trustworthy answer it can find.

## GEO vs SEO

| | SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank in the list of results | Be cited in the AI answer |
| Unit | A whole page | A passage, fact or definition |
| Signals | Relevance, links, page experience | Clear answers, evidence, trust, freshness |
| Measure | Rankings, clicks | Mentions, citations, referral visits |

They overlap far more than they differ. A page that is slow, blocked from crawling or thin will not do well in either.

## What Google says about AI Overviews

Google's guidance on [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) is clear on several points:

- There are **no additional requirements** to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. A page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet.
- You **do not need new machine-readable files**, special "AI text files" or new markup for Google.
- Normal SEO basics apply: allow crawling, use internal links, offer a good page experience and keep structured data consistent with the visible text.
- Clicks from AI features are counted in Search Console's Performance report under the "Web" search type.

So, for Google, generative engine optimization is mostly good SEO done well. Other AI tools have their own crawlers, so check that your site does not block the ones you want.

## How to optimize for AI answers

@img chart

1. **Answer the question first.** Put a direct, two or three sentence answer at the top of the page or section.
2. **Use clear headings** that match how people ask: "How much does a roof repair cost in Denver?"
3. **Back claims with sources.** Cite official data and original research, and add the date you checked it.
4. **Add specific facts.** Prices, steps, timelines and examples are easier to quote than general claims.
5. **Use structured data** that matches the page, such as Organization, LocalBusiness, Article and FAQ. Our free [schema markup generator](/tools/schema-markup-generator/) helps.
6. **Show who is behind the content.** Author names, credentials, an about page and real contact details build trust.
7. **Keep pages fresh.** Update prices, rules and screenshots at least once a year.
8. **Earn mentions elsewhere.** Reviews, local press and industry directories help AI tools trust your brand.

## Do you need an llms.txt file?

An llms.txt file is a short summary of your site's most useful pages, written for AI tools. Google says it does not need one, and support among other AI tools is uneven. It is cheap to add and harmless, so some sites publish one as a simple map. Our free [llms.txt generator](/tools/llms-txt-generator/) builds one in minutes. Do not expect it to replace good pages.

## Generative AI in marketing: use it carefully

Generative AI can help draft outlines, summaries and first versions of pages. But AI tools favor accurate, specific, trustworthy sources. Content written by AI and published without checks tends to be generic, and it can contain errors. Use AI for speed and people for facts and judgment. ## How to measure GEO

- **Search Console:** watch impressions and clicks for question-style queries.
- **Referral traffic:** check analytics for visits from AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and Gemini.
- **Manual checks:** ask AI tools your main customer questions each month and note who gets cited.
- **Brand searches:** a rise in people searching your name can follow AI mentions.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is generative engine optimization?
Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making your content easy for AI answer engines to find, understand and cite. It builds on SEO with a focus on clear answers, evidence and trust.

? Is GEO different from SEO?
It overlaps heavily. Google says pages need no special work beyond being indexed and eligible for snippets. The difference is focus: GEO pays extra attention to quotable answers, sources and brand trust.

? How do I get my business into Google's AI Overviews?
Make sure your pages can be crawled and indexed, answer common questions clearly, keep structured data accurate and build trust with reviews and real business details. Google does not offer a way to request inclusion.

? Does AI search reduce website traffic?
It can reduce clicks for simple questions that the answer fully covers. Google says clicks from AI Overviews tend to be higher quality. Pages with depth, tools, prices and local details still earn visits.
