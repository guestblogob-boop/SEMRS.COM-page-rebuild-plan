An advertising budget is the amount you plan to spend on ads over a month or year, and how you split it across channels. Too little and campaigns never get enough data to work; too much too soon and you pay to learn expensive lessons. This guide shows how to set an advertising budget by working back from what a customer is worth, then testing, measuring and scaling.

## Key takeaways

- Start from customer value: how much you can afford to pay to win one customer.
- Fund one or two channels properly for at least a month before judging them.
- Judge ads by cost per lead and cost per customer, then move budget to what works.

## Set your advertising budget in 6 steps

@img chart

### 1. Work out what a customer is worth

Estimate the profit from an average customer over a year or their lifetime. A cleaning client worth $150 profit a month for 12 months is worth $1,800 a year.

### 2. Decide what you can pay to win one

Pick a target cost per customer that leaves a healthy profit, for example a quarter of the first-year profit. In the example above, that is $450.

### 3. Work back to a monthly budget

| Step | Example |
|---|---|
| Customers wanted per month | 4 |
| Target cost per customer | $450 |
| Monthly ad budget | $1,800 |
| Leads needed if 1 in 4 becomes a customer | 16 |
| Target cost per lead | $112 |

These are example numbers, not benchmarks. Replace them with your own.

### 4. Choose one or two channels

Pick the channels where your customers are actively looking. Local services usually start with Google search ads and Local Services Ads; visual products often start with Meta ads. Our guides to [Google Ads for small business](/google-ads-for-small-business/) and [Local Services Ads vs Google Ads](/local-services-ads-vs-google-ads/) help you choose.

### 5. Run a test month

Give each channel enough budget to collect data for at least a month. On Google Ads, remember that your daily budget is an average: Google's help page says a campaign can spend up to twice its daily budget on a busy day but will not charge more than 30.4 times the daily budget in a month.

### 6. Measure and adjust

Track leads and customers from each channel. Keep what reaches your target cost per customer, fix what is close and cut what clearly fails. Our free [ROAS calculator](/tools/roas-calculator/) compares spend with revenue.

## Ad spend vs total advertising costs

Your advertising budget should include more than the money paid to platforms:

- **Ad spend** paid to Google, Meta or other platforms.
- **Management fees** if an agency or freelancer runs the ads.
- **Creative costs** for design, video or copywriting.
- **Tools** such as call tracking or landing page software.

Our guide to [digital marketing cost](/how-much-does-digital-marketing-cost/) lists typical pricing models.

## Common advertising budget mistakes

- **Spreading a small budget across many channels.**
- **Stopping after a week**, before campaigns have enough data.
- **No conversion tracking**, so nobody knows what works.
- **Counting clicks instead of customers.**
- **Raising budgets on campaigns that are not yet profitable.**

## Seasonal budgets

Many businesses have busy and quiet seasons. Spend more when customers are searching, such as heating repairs in winter, and keep a smaller, steady budget in quiet months to stay visible.

## Frequently asked questions

? How much should a small business spend on advertising?
There is no single right amount. Work back from what a customer is worth and how many you want each month, then test that budget for at least a month.

? What should an advertising budget include?
Ad spend, management fees, creative costs and tools such as call tracking. Budgeting only for ad spend understates the real cost.

? How long should I test a new ad channel?
Give it at least a month with enough budget to collect leads, then judge it by cost per lead and cost per customer.

? Can Google Ads spend more than my daily budget?
Yes, on some days. Google says a campaign can spend up to twice its average daily budget in a day, but it will not charge more than 30.4 times the daily budget in a month.
