Website design cost for a small business ranges from a few dollars a month for a do-it-yourself builder to several thousand dollars for a custom site. The right budget depends on how many pages you need, who writes the content and whether you sell online. This guide breaks down the options, the running costs that quotes often leave out and how to compare quotes fairly.

## Key takeaways

- Compare quotes by what is included: pages, copywriting, SEO setup, revisions and support after launch.
- Budget for running costs too: domain, hosting, updates and small edits each year.
- Own your domain, hosting and site files, so you can change designers without starting again.

## What decides the cost of a website

Five things drive most of the price:

1. **Number of pages.** A one-page site costs far less than 25 service and location pages.
2. **Copywriting.** Writing good pages takes as long as designing them. Check whether the quote includes it.
3. **Design.** A tuned template costs less than a fully custom design.
4. **Features.** Online booking, a store, a second language or member areas all add work.
5. **SEO and speed.** Titles, schema, sitemaps and Core Web Vitals checks are often extras.

## Website design cost by option

| Option | Typical cost | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| DIY website builder | About $12 to $49 a month on annual billing | Simple sites, tight budgets | Your time, design limits, moving away later |
| Freelancer | Varies widely by skill and location | Clear, small projects | Availability, support after launch |
| Small agency | One-off project fee | A finished site with copy and SEO | Scope creep, who owns the site |
| Custom build | Quoted per project | Portals, apps, complex stores | Long timelines, higher upkeep |

Builder prices are from the vendors' own pages in November 2026: Squarespace plans start at $12 a month and Wix plans at $17 a month on annual billing, while Shopify starts at $19 a month for online stores. Prices change often, so check before you sign up. If you plan to build it yourself, our guide to [SEO for small business](/seo-for-small-business/) covers the setup that helps a new site get found.

## Running costs most quotes leave out

- **Domain name:** a yearly fee paid to your registrar.
- **Hosting:** a monthly or yearly fee paid to your host. Cheap hosting can slow the site down.
- **Updates and backups:** WordPress, plugins and themes need regular updates.
- **Edits and new pages:** prices, staff and services change.
- **Paid plugins or apps:** forms, booking or store features may carry yearly licenses.

A care plan bundles updates, backups and small edits for a monthly fee. At SEMRS, care plans start at $39 a month.

## Website redesign cost

A redesign usually costs about the same as a new site with the same pages, because most of the work is content and design. It can cost less when you keep your content and only change the look. Before a redesign, list your pages that get search traffic. Keep their addresses, or set redirects, so you do not lose rankings.

## How to compare website design quotes

1. **Write a short brief:** goals, pages, features and examples you like.
2. **Ask each designer to price the same brief**, so quotes are comparable.
3. **Check who writes the copy** and how many revision rounds are included.
4. **Ask about speed and accessibility.** Google's [Core Web Vitals guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals) sets clear targets for loading, response and layout stability.
5. **Confirm ownership** of the domain, hosting account and site files.
6. **Ask what happens after launch:** support period, training and the cost of later edits.

## Frequently asked questions

? How much does a website cost for a small business?
A simple site can cost a monthly builder fee if you build it yourself. A professionally designed small business site with copywriting usually costs a one-off project fee, from a few hundred to a few thousand dollars, plus yearly running costs.

? What is a fair price for website design?
A fair price matches the scope: pages, copy, features and support. Ask several designers to price the same brief and compare what is included, not just the total.

? Is it cheaper to build a website yourself?
It costs less money but more time. A builder works well for a simple site. If your site needs to rank in search and bring leads, professional setup often pays back.

? How much does website maintenance cost each year?
Expect to pay for your domain, hosting, updates and occasional edits. A care plan bundles these into one monthly fee.
