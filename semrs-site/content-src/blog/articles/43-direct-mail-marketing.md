Direct mail marketing means sending printed postcards, letters or flyers to people's homes or businesses. In a world of crowded inboxes, a well-targeted postcard can stand out, especially for local services such as cleaners, dentists, restaurants and home improvement companies. This guide explains when direct mail works, how to target it, what it costs and how to track results.

## Key takeaways

- Direct mail works best for local businesses with a clear offer and a defined area.
- USPS Every Door Direct Mail lets you reach every address on chosen routes without a mailing list.
- Track every piece with a unique phone number, QR code or web address.

## When direct mail marketing works

- **Local service businesses** that serve specific neighborhoods.
- **New businesses** announcing an opening.
- **Seasonal offers** such as lawn care in spring or heating checks in autumn.
- **Past customers** who need a reminder to book again.
- **High-value services** where one new customer pays for the whole mailing.

It works less well for online-only businesses selling to a wide, scattered audience.

## Ways to target direct mail

| Method | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Every Door Direct Mail (EDDM) | Mail to every address on chosen postal routes | Neighborhood saturation for local businesses |
| Customer list | Mail to your own past customers | Re-booking and loyalty offers |
| Rented list | Mail to addresses chosen by traits such as home ownership | Targeted offers outside your current customers |

### USPS Every Door Direct Mail

EDDM mail is addressed to "Postal Customer" and delivered to every address on the routes you pick. On the USPS page in November 2026, EDDM Retail flats were listed at $0.26 per piece, with a minimum of 200 and a maximum of 5,000 pieces per day per ZIP Code. Printing and design are extra.

## Run a direct mail campaign in 7 steps

@img chart

1. **Choose the area** where your best customers live or where you want to grow.
2. **Pick one offer**, such as a free inspection, a first-visit discount or a seasonal service.
3. **Design for a two-second glance:** a bold headline, one image, the offer and how to respond.
4. **Add tracking:** a unique phone number, a QR code to a dedicated landing page or a short web address.
5. **Print and send**, timed for when people need your service.
6. **Repeat** to the same area. People often respond after seeing the same message several times.
7. **Measure** calls, visits and bookings against the cost of the mailing.

## Design tips that lift response

- Put the offer in the headline, not the small print.
- Use a real photo of your team or work.
- Include your phone number, website and a deadline.
- Keep text short and easy to read.
- Add reviews or star ratings only if they are real.

## Combine direct mail with online marketing

Direct mail works better alongside online channels. People who get a postcard often search your name before calling, so make sure your [Google Business Profile](/google-business-profile-optimization/) and reviews look strong. Send the QR code to a page about the exact offer, not your home page. Our guide to [digital marketing strategy](/digital-marketing-strategy/) shows how to fit mail into a wider plan.

## What direct mail costs

Your total cost is design, printing and postage. Postage for EDDM was listed at $0.26 per piece on the USPS site in November 2026, while printing depends on size, paper and quantity. Work out how many customers you need to break even: divide the total cost by the profit from one new customer.
