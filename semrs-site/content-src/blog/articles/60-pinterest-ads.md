Pinterest ads reach people while they plan: a kitchen remodel, a wedding, a new wardrobe, a holiday menu. That planning mindset makes Pinterest a good fit for businesses with visual products and ideas, from home decor and recipes to fashion, events and DIY. This guide covers campaign objectives, ad formats, targeting, creative that works and how to measure results.

## Key takeaways

- Pinterest suits visual, plan-ahead purchases; people save ideas before they buy.
- Pinterest Business lists three goals: awareness, consideration, and leads and sales.
- Use tall, clear images with your product in use, and send clicks to a matching page.

## Is Pinterest right for your business?

Pinterest works best when people look for ideas before they buy:

| Good fit | Weaker fit |
|---|---|
| Home decor, furniture, renovation | Emergency services |
| Recipes, food and drink | Very local, urgent needs |
| Fashion, beauty, jewelry | Products with nothing visual to show |
| Weddings, events, parties | Complex B2B services |
| DIY, crafts, gardening | |

## Campaign goals and formats

@img chart

Pinterest's business site groups campaigns by goal:

1. **Awareness:** reach people who do not know your brand yet.
2. **Consideration:** get people to click through to your site.
3. **Leads and sales:** drive sign-ups, enquiries or purchases.

It names formats including **image ads**, **Idea ads** and **Premiere Spotlight** video placements. Check Pinterest Ads Manager for the formats available to your account and country, as they change over time.

## Targeting on Pinterest

- **Keywords:** what people search on Pinterest, such as "small kitchen ideas".
- **Interests:** topics people engage with.
- **Audiences:** your website visitors, customer lists you have permission to use, and people similar to them.
- **Location, language and device.**

Housing, credit and employment ads have extra rules on most platforms, so check Pinterest's ad policies before targeting.

## Creative that works on Pinterest

- **Tall images** that stand out in the feed.
- **Show the product in use:** the finished room, the plated dish, the outfit on a person.
- **Add a short text overlay** that names the idea, such as "Small kitchen storage ideas".
- **Keep your brand visible** but subtle.
- **Match the landing page** to the image so people find what they clicked on.

## Pinterest ads vs other platforms

Google search ads reach people ready to buy now; Instagram ads interrupt scrolling with attention-grabbing content; Pinterest reaches people planning. Many visual businesses use Pinterest for planning-stage reach and Google for ready buyers. Our guide to [Instagram ads](/instagram-ads/) covers Meta's formats.
