Instagram ads cost what you decide to spend: you set a daily or lifetime budget, and Meta's ad auction decides how far that budget goes. There is no fixed price per ad. What you pay per thousand views or per click depends on your audience, your goal, the time of year and how good your ad is. This guide explains how the pricing works and how to set a budget you can trust.

## Key takeaways

- You control spend with a daily or lifetime budget; the auction sets the price of each result.
- Meta picks the winning ad by total value: the bid, estimated action rates and ad quality.
- Judge Instagram ads by cost per lead or sale, not by the cost of a click.

## How Meta's auction sets the price

Instagram ads are sold through Meta's ad auction, the same system used for Facebook ads, which is why Facebook and Instagram ads cost is usually discussed together. Meta says the winner of each auction is the ad with the highest total value, which combines three factors:

- **Bid:** what you are willing to pay for your goal.
- **Estimated action rates:** how likely the person is to engage or convert.
- **Ad quality:** how relevant and well made the ad is.

A better, more relevant ad can win more often at a lower price. That is why creative quality matters as much as budget.

## The pricing terms you will see

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CPM | Cost per 1,000 impressions (views of your ad) |
| CPC | Cost per click on your ad |
| CPL | Cost per lead, such as a form or call |
| CPA | Cost per action, such as a sale |
| ROAS | Revenue for every dollar of ad spend |

The numbers below are an example, not a benchmark. Your costs will differ by audience, goal and season.

1. **Budget:** $10 a day for 30 days = $300.
2. **Example CPM of $8:** $300 ÷ $8 × 1,000 = 37,500 impressions.
3. **Example click rate of 1%:** about 375 clicks, or $0.80 a click.
4. **Example 5% of clicks become leads:** about 19 leads, or about $16 a lead.

## What raises or lowers Instagram ad costs

| Raises costs | Lowers costs |
|---|---|
| Small, very competitive audiences | Broader audiences with a clear offer |
| Busy seasons such as the holidays | Quieter months |
| Weak or repeated creative | Fresh, relevant images and video |
| Goals far down the funnel at first | Enough data for the algorithm to learn |
| Slow or confusing landing pages | Fast pages that match the ad |

## 5 steps to control your Instagram ads cost

1. **Pick one goal** per campaign: leads, sales or traffic.
2. **Start small,** with a daily budget you can test for two to four weeks.
3. **Make two or three versions** of the ad and keep the best.
4. **Track results on your website** with conversion tracking, so you see cost per lead or sale.
5. **Scale slowly,** raising the budget gradually on what works.

## Frequently asked questions

? How much do Instagram ads cost?
You choose the budget. The auction then sets the price of each impression, click or lead, which varies by audience, goal, season and ad quality.

? Is there a minimum budget for Instagram ads?
Yes. Meta requires a minimum budget that varies by country, objective and other settings, and Ads Manager alerts you if your budget is too low.

? Are Instagram ads cheaper than Facebook ads?
Neither is always cheaper. Both use the same auction, so test both placements and compare cost per result.

? How can I lower my Instagram ads cost?
Use a clear offer, fresh creative, a broad enough audience and a fast landing page, then let the campaign learn before changing it.
