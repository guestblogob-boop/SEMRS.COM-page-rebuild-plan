An email campaign is a planned series of emails with one goal, such as filling a workshop, launching a service or bringing back past customers. Done well, it is one of the cheapest ways to turn your list into sales. This guide shows how to plan one in eight steps, what to write, how to stay out of spam and which rules every sender must follow.

## Key takeaways

- Give each campaign one goal, one audience and one clear call to action.
- Plan two to four emails over one to three weeks rather than a single send.
- Follow CAN-SPAM, authenticate your domain and track clicks to your site.

## Plan an email campaign in 8 steps

@img chart

### 1. Set one goal

"Book 30 boiler services in March" is better than "send a newsletter". The goal decides the audience, offer and success measure.

### 2. Choose the audience

Send to the people the offer suits: past customers, new subscribers, people who clicked a topic before. Smaller, relevant lists get better results than sending everything to everyone.

### 3. Shape the offer

Give a reason to act now: a seasonal service, a limited number of slots or a useful guide. Keep it honest; no fake deadlines.

### 4. Write the sequence

A simple three-email campaign:

1. **Announcement:** the offer and why it matters.
2. **Reminder:** answer common questions or share a customer story.
3. **Last chance:** a short note before the offer ends.

### 5. Write subject lines that earn the open

Keep subject lines clear and specific: "Your boiler check: 20 slots left for March". Match the subject to the email; misleading subjects break the law and the reader's trust.

### 6. Design for phones

Use one column, short paragraphs, a large button and alt text on images. Most people read email on their phone.

### 7. Test before sending

Send a test to yourself, check links, check the mobile view and preview the plain-text version.

### 8. Send, then measure

Track opens, clicks, replies, unsubscribes and, most important, bookings or sales. Tag links with UTM codes using our free [UTM builder](/tools/utm-builder/) so visits show up in your analytics.

## Rules you must follow

The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide sets the rules for US commercial email. Every campaign email must:

- Use accurate "From" details and a subject line that is not deceptive.
- Identify itself as an ad where it is one.
- Include a valid postal address.
- Offer a clear way to opt out, and honor opt-outs within 10 business days.

Google's email sender guidelines also require senders to authenticate their domain. Bulk senders to Gmail must set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC, offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaints low.

## Frequently asked questions

? How many emails should an email campaign have?
Most small business campaigns work well with two to four emails over one to three weeks: an announcement, a reminder and a last-chance note.

? What is a good click rate?
It varies by industry and list. Compare each campaign with your own past results and focus on bookings or sales, which matter more than opens.

? When is the best time to send?
Test with your own list. Many businesses find weekday mornings work, but your audience's habits matter more than general advice.

? Do I need permission to send marketing emails?
In the US, CAN-SPAM allows commercial email if you follow its rules, including an easy opt-out. Many other countries require consent first, so collect clear opt-ins.
