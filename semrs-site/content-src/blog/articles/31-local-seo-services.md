Local SEO services help a business show up when nearby customers search, in the Google map results and in the normal results below them. For plumbers, dentists, lawyers, salons and other local businesses, that is where most new customers come from. This guide explains what good local SEO includes, how they are priced and how to tell a real provider from a poor one.

## Key takeaways

- Good local SEO covers your Business Profile, service and location pages, reviews, citations and reporting on calls and leads.
- Google says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking, so be wary of anyone who claims otherwise.
- Ask for a written scope, monthly reports from your own accounts and a contract you can leave.

[glance: Local SEO services at a glance | Profile and reviews | Service area pages | Clear monthly reports | No paid ranking promises]

## How local rankings work

Google's help page on local ranking says local results are based mainly on three things: **relevance** (how well your profile matches the search), **distance** (how far you are from the searcher or the place in the search) and **prominence** (how well known your business is, including reviews and ratings). It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Local SEO work focuses on relevance and prominence. Distance you cannot change, but clear service-area pages help you show for nearby towns you really serve.

## Questions to ask a local SEO provider

1. **What exactly will you do each month?**
2. **Who owns the Business Profile, website and accounts?** It should be you.
3. **How will you report results?** Look for calls, leads and map views, not only rankings.
4. **How do you get reviews?** The answer should follow Google's and the FTC's rules.
5. **What is the contract length and notice period?**
6. **Can you show examples from similar local businesses?**

## Warning signs

- **Guaranteed number one rankings.**
- **Fake reviews or paid reviews.** They break Google's rules and the FTC's review rule.
- **Fake addresses** or virtual offices to appear in more towns.
- **Keyword-stuffed business names** on your profile, which can get it suspended.
- **Reports with no leads or calls**, only rankings for phrases nobody searches.
- **The provider owns your profile** or website.

## Do it yourself or hire help?

You can do much of local SEO yourself: complete your profile, ask for reviews and build good service pages. Our guide to [SEO for small business](/seo-for-small-business/) shows the steps. Hiring makes sense when you lack time, have several locations or face strong local competition.

## Frequently asked questions

? What do local SEO services include?
They usually include Google Business Profile optimization, service and location pages, a review strategy, citations, local schema, technical fixes, local links and monthly reporting on calls and leads.

? How much does local SEO cost?
Most providers charge a monthly retainer based on the number of locations and pages. Compare what is delivered each month, not just the price.

? How long does local SEO take to work?
Profile and review improvements can show results within weeks. New pages and links usually take three to six months to have a full effect.

? Can I pay Google to rank higher in Maps?
No. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. You can pay for ads that appear above or within map results, which are labeled as ads.
