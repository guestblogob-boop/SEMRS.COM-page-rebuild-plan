What is SEO?

Search engine optimization, explained in plain words

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the work that helps your website appear when people search Google for what you sell. It has four parts: making sure Google can crawl and index your pages (technical SEO), making each page clearly answer one search (on-page SEO), showing up in local results and Google Maps (local SEO), and publishing helpful content that earns visits and links (content SEO).

Unlike ads, you do not pay for each click. Good SEO keeps bringing visitors months after the work is done, which is why it is usually the lowest-cost source of leads for small businesses over time.

How each part works

A closer look at our SEO work

Technical SEO makes sure search engines can crawl, render and index every important page quickly. We start every project with a technical SEO audit and fix the issues in order of impact.

On-page SEO aligns each page with one search intent. We improve what Google and visitors see first, then strengthen internal links so authority flows to your money pages.

Local SEO helps nearby customers find you in Google Maps and the local pack. It matters for any business that serves a city or region.

Content SEO builds the topical authority that helps whole sections of your site rank. GEO (generative engine optimization) makes the same content easy for AI assistants to quote.
