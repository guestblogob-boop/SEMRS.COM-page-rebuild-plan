Why estimate before you invest

Marketing proposals often promise growth without showing how it turns into revenue. A simple model makes the conversation clearer: how many extra visitors, how many become enquiries, how many become customers, and what each customer is worth. Once you see the chain, it is easier to judge whether a budget makes sense and which part of your business to improve first.

The numbers that matter

  • Traffic: how many relevant visitors you get now and how much demand exists.
  • Conversion rate: the share of visitors who call, book, buy or enquire.
  • Close rate: the share of enquiries that become customers.
  • Customer value: revenue or profit per customer, including repeat business where relevant.
  • Cost and time: what you invest and how long results take to build.

Using results responsibly

Calculators estimate; they do not predict. Real outcomes depend on your market, competition, website and how well enquiries are handled. Be wary of anyone who guarantees rankings or traffic. Use these tools to plan, compare and ask better questions.

Tips for local businesses

For local service businesses in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, organic search often brings fewer visitors than national websites see, but those visitors are usually ready to buy: they want a service in their area now. That is why conversion and close rates for local searches are often high. When you use the calculator, count phone calls as leads, use a realistic customer value that includes repeat work, and run separate scenarios for each genuine service area rather than one large total.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my conversion rate?

Divide enquiries or sales from a channel by visits from that channel over the same period. Track phone calls as well as forms.

Should I use revenue or profit?

Revenue shows the size of the opportunity; profit shows whether it is worthwhile. Try both.

How long until SEO pays back?

It varies widely by competition and starting point. Many businesses see meaningful progress within four to twelve months.
