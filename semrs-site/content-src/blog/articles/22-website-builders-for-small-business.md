Website builders for small business let you launch a professional site without writing code. The main choices are WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and Shopify, and each suits a different kind of business. This guide compares them on cost, ownership, SEO and upkeep, and gives you a simple way to choose the right one for your plans.

## Key takeaways

- Hosted builders such as Wix and Squarespace are quick to start and need little upkeep, but your site lives on their platform.
- WordPress gives you full ownership and flexibility, with more upkeep or a care plan.
- Shopify is built for selling online; choose it when the store is the business.

## The four main options

**WordPress** is free, open-source software that you install on your own hosting. It runs about 38% of all websites, according to W3Techs, and offers thousands of themes and plugins. You own the files and can move hosts at any time.

**Wix** is a hosted, drag-and-drop builder. Hosting, security and updates are included in the plan. It is easy for beginners and has apps for bookings, stores and events.

**Squarespace** is a hosted builder known for polished templates. It suits portfolios, service businesses and small shops that value design and simplicity.

**Shopify** is a hosted e-commerce platform. It handles products, checkout, payments, shipping and taxes, and it scales from a few products to large catalogs.

## Cost: what you really pay

Look past the headline price. Check what plan you need for a custom domain, online payments, bookings and removing platform ads. On Squarespace, for example, the Basic plan charges a 2% fee on store sales while the Core plan charges none. Shopify starts with a free trial and a low first-months offer, then switches to the normal monthly price.

With WordPress, you pay for hosting, a domain and any premium plugins. Many small businesses also pay for updates and backups, either their own time or a care plan. Our guide to [website design cost](/website-design-cost/) lists the running costs to budget for.

## SEO and speed

All four can rank well when the content is good. The differences are in control:

- **WordPress** gives full control over titles, schema, redirects, page speed and site structure, with SEO plugins.
- **Wix and Squarespace** cover the basics, such as titles, descriptions and sitemaps, with less control over code and speed.
- **Shopify** handles product SEO well; some URL structures are fixed by the platform.

Whatever you choose, keep pages fast on phones. Our guide to [SEO for small business](/seo-for-small-business/) covers what matters most.

## Accessibility and ownership

Choose a template that works with a keyboard and has good color contrast. Our [ADA website compliance guide](/ada-website-compliance/) explains the standard.

Ownership matters more than most owners expect. On a hosted builder, you usually cannot take the design with you if you leave. Make sure your domain is registered in your own name, whatever builder you use.

## How to choose a website builder

1. **Define the main goal:** leads, bookings, content or online sales.
2. **Count the pages and features** you need in the next two years, not just today.
3. **Decide who will maintain it:** you, a staff member or a professional.
4. **Price the plan you will really need**, including apps and fees.
5. **Test the editor** with a free trial before you commit.
6. **Plan your exit:** check what you can export if you move later.

As a rule of thumb, choose Wix or Squarespace for a simple site you will run yourself, Shopify for a store and WordPress for a site that needs to grow, rank and stay fully yours.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is the best website builder for a small business?
It depends on your goal. Wix and Squarespace suit simple sites you manage yourself, Shopify suits online stores and WordPress suits businesses that want full control, strong SEO and ownership.

? Is WordPress better than Wix for SEO?
WordPress gives more control over technical SEO, speed and site structure. Wix covers the basics well. For most small sites, content quality and local SEO matter more than the platform.

? Can I move my website from Wix to WordPress later?
Yes, but it is usually a rebuild. You can copy your text and images, but the design does not transfer. Plan redirects so you keep your search rankings.

? How much does a website builder cost per month?
Small business plans on hosted builders start at about $12 to $19 a month on annual billing in November 2026. WordPress software is free, but hosting, a domain and upkeep add to the cost.
