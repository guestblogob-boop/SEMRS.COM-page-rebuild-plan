AI market research means using AI tools to collect, summarize and make sense of information about your customers, competitors and market. It can turn hundreds of reviews into clear themes in minutes. But AI can also invent numbers, so the facts must come from real sources. This guide shows six practical steps, the free data sources to pair with AI and the checks every result needs.

## Key takeaways

- Use AI to organize and summarize real data, not to invent market facts.
- Pair it with free government data, such as the Census Bureau and Bureau of Labor Statistics, and your own customer feedback.
- Check every number against its source before you act on it.

## What market research covers

The SBA describes market research as helping you find customers for your business. It combines existing data, such as industry trends, demographics and household incomes, with research you do yourself through surveys, questionnaires, focus groups and interviews.

## Six steps to AI market research

@img chart

1. **Write the question.** "Who buys home cleaning in our city, and what do they value?" is better than "research the market".
2. **Gather real sources.** Census data, industry reports, your reviews, competitor reviews, survey answers and sales notes.
3. **Use AI to summarize.** Paste survey answers or reviews and ask for the most common themes, complaints and phrases.
4. **Use AI to compare.** Ask it to compare competitors' service pages and list the differences in offers, prices shown and guarantees.
5. **Check every fact** against the original source. Delete anything you cannot confirm.
6. **Decide and test.** Turn findings into one or two actions, such as a new service page or offer, and measure the result.

## Free data sources to use with AI

The SBA's market research guide lists free sources, including:

| Source | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| U.S. Census Bureau (data.census.gov) | Population, households and income by area |
| Census Business Builder | Customer and business data for a location |
| Statistics of U.S. Businesses | Number of businesses by industry and size |
| Bureau of Labor Statistics | Employment, wages and prices |
| Bureau of Economic Analysis | Economic output and spending |

Download the data, then ask AI to summarize the parts that matter for your area.

## Prompts that work

- "Here are 120 customer reviews. Group the praise and complaints into themes, with counts and example quotes."
- "Compare these three competitor service pages. List what each offers, the guarantees they make and any prices they show."
- "Here are our survey answers. What questions do customers ask most before buying?"

## The limits of AI for market research

- **It can invent statistics and sources.** Never publish an AI number without checking it.
- **Its knowledge may be out of date.**
- **It reflects the data you give it.** Biased inputs give biased answers.
- **Do not paste personal customer data** into consumer AI tools.

## Frequently asked questions

? Can AI do market research?
AI can organize and summarize research quickly, such as reviews, surveys and competitor pages. The facts must come from real sources, and a person should check them.

? What free data can I use for market research?
The SBA lists free sources such as the U.S. Census Bureau, Census Business Builder, Statistics of U.S. Businesses, the Bureau of Labor Statistics and the Bureau of Economic Analysis.

? Is AI market research accurate?
Only as accurate as the data you give it and the checks you make. AI can produce confident but wrong numbers, so verify every figure.

? How can AI help with competitor research?
Paste competitors' public pages and reviews and ask AI to compare offers, guarantees and common complaints.
