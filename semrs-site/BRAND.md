# SEMRS Brand Brief — DRAFT v0.1 (awaiting approval)

> Draft based on the recommended positioning (decision D1). Final once `SEMRS_AGENCY_STRATEGY.md` is reviewed and you approve.

## Positioning

**One line:** SEO-led growth for small businesses, with prices in public and a human approving every deliverable.

**Core services:** SEO (incl. technical, content, audit, GEO/AI search), Web Design, Content Management.
**Second tier:** Paid Ads, Social Media, Lead Generation, Email & WhatsApp Marketing, Website Care.

**Who it's for:** small and growing businesses that want organic leads without agency jargon, long contracts or hidden pricing.

## Proof we can honestly use

- Public from-prices (from the OS pricing catalog)
- 6-step process with a CEO approval gate
- Reports built only from real Search Console and GA4 data
- Free tools anyone can use without signing up
- Our own site's Core Web Vitals scores (measured, dated)

## Tone of voice

Plain, direct, calm. Short sentences. Second person ("you"). Explain the outcome first, the method second. Confident without hype.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "From $X/month. Cancel with 30 days' notice." | "Affordable packages tailored to you!" |
| "We fix what stops Google indexing your pages." | "We leverage cutting-edge synergies." |
| "Results depend on your market. Here's what we control." | "Guaranteed #1 rankings." |

## Words to use
clear, measured, approved, real data, from-price, plan, report, fix, publish, leads, enquiries

## Words to avoid
guaranteed, #1, best in the world, cutting-edge, revolutionary, synergy, skyrocket, 10x, hack, secret, "millions of visitors" (unless measured and dated)

## Banned claims (hard rules)

1. No ranking, traffic or revenue guarantees.
2. No invented numbers (clients served, visitors, ROI, years) — only figures with a source and a date.
3. No testimonials, reviews, logos or case studies without written permission and evidence.
4. No credentials, partner badges or awards SEMRS doesn't hold.
5. No implied affiliation with Semrush or any tool vendor.
6. No paid dofollow links; sponsored content uses `rel="sponsored"`.

## Visual identity (to finalise in the child theme step)

- Colours: navy (primary) and gold (accent) from the S-monogram; white backgrounds; one neutral grey scale.
- Type: Inter, self-hosted. One bold headline per screen.
- Imagery: real founder photography; no stock "team" photos; diagrams over decoration.
- Motion: only where it explains something.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast on every text/background pair.
