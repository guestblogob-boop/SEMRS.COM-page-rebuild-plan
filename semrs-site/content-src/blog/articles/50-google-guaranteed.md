The Google Guaranteed and Google Screened badges appear on Local Services Ads, the paid listings that show above normal search results for many local services. They tell searchers that Google has checked the business. This guide explains what each badge means, which businesses get which one, the checks involved and how to decide whether it is worth it for you.

## Key takeaways

- Both badges come with Local Services Ads; home services usually get Google Guaranteed, professional services usually get Google Screened.
- Businesses pass Google's screening, which can include license, insurance and background checks; Google says it takes three to four weeks on average.
- The badge builds trust, but reviews, fast replies and a good profile still decide who gets the call.

## What the badges mean

| | Google Guaranteed | Google Screened |
|---|---|---|
| Typical businesses | Home services such as plumbers, electricians and cleaners | Professional services such as lawyers, financial planners and real estate agents |
| What it signals | The business passed Google's checks, backed by Google's guarantee program for eligible customers | The business passed Google's checks |
| Where it shows | Local Services Ads | Local Services Ads |

Google's help pages say providers are verified through Google's own screening process. For some categories, such as electricians, this includes background checks on service professionals. Verification can include business and owner licenses at state level. Lawyers must also pass state bar license checks. Google says real estate agents receive the Google Screened badge based on its background check requirement.

The guarantee program has its own terms, limits and claim rules, which Google can change. Read the current terms on Google's Local Services help center before you mention it to customers.

## How to get the Google Guaranteed badge

1. **Check eligibility:** your category and area must be supported by Local Services Ads.
2. **Create a Local Services Ads profile** with your services, hours and service area.
3. **Submit documents:** licenses and insurance as required for your category and state.
4. **Complete background checks** where your category needs them.
5. **Wait for screening.** Google says this takes three to four weeks on average after you submit documents.
6. **Set a budget** and start receiving leads.

## Is it worth it?

The badge helps when people compare several businesses in a hurry. Searchers see the check mark, your star rating and how quickly you respond. It is most useful for:

- Home services that get urgent calls.
- Businesses with strong reviews.
- Teams that can answer calls quickly.

It helps less if you cannot answer leads quickly or your reviews are weak. Fix those first.

## Frequently asked questions

? What is Google Guaranteed?
It is a badge shown on Local Services Ads for eligible businesses, mostly home services, that have passed Google's screening. It is backed by Google's guarantee program, which has its own terms.

? What is the difference between Google Guaranteed and Google Screened?
Google Guaranteed is mainly for home services; Google Screened is for professional services such as lawyers and financial planners. Both mean the business passed Google's checks.

? How long does Google screening take?
Google says it takes three to four weeks on average after you submit your documents.

? Does the badge improve my ranking in Google Maps?
The badge appears in Local Services Ads, not in the free map results. Your map ranking still depends on relevance, distance and prominence.
