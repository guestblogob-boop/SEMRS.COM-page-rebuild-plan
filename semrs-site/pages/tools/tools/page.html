How to choose the right free SEO tools

Start with the problem you are trying to solve:

  • Your pages do not get clicked in Google. Use the SERP Snippet Preview and the Meta Title & Description Checker to write titles and descriptions that fit and stand out.
  • Pages are missing from Google, or you changed your website. Use the Robots.txt Generator to check crawler rules and the Redirect Checker to make sure old URLs lead to the right new pages.
  • You want search engines and AI tools to understand your business. Use the Schema Markup Generator, the Local Business Schema Generator and the llms.txt Generator.
  • You are writing or improving content. Use the Word Counter to check length, readability and keyword use.
  • You need more reviews. Use the Google Review Link Generator to give customers a one-tap review link.
  • You want to know which marketing works. Use the UTM Builder to track campaigns and the SEO ROI Calculator to estimate the value of organic traffic.
  • Your website is slow. Use the Image Compressor & WebP Converter to shrink images without uploading them anywhere.
