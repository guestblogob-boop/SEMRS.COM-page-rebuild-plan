Healthcare SEO helps clinics, practices and health providers appear when people search for care, symptoms and treatments. Health is one of the topics Google treats with the most care, because wrong information can cause real harm. That means trust, accuracy and real expertise count for more here than almost anywhere else.

## Why healthcare SEO is different

Google's guidance on helpful content explains that its systems give extra weight to strong E-E-A-T (experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness) for topics that could significantly affect people's health, financial stability or safety. Google calls these "Your Money or Your Life" topics. Of the four parts of E-E-A-T, Google says trust is the most important.

## 8 essential healthcare SEO steps

1. **Claim and complete your Google Business Profile** for each location, with hours, services and accepted insurance.
2. **Build one page per service or treatment,** answering what it is, who it helps, what to expect, risks and costs.
3. **Show who wrote and reviewed each page,** with credentials and a "last reviewed" date.
4. **Cite trusted sources** such as health agencies and peer-reviewed research.
5. **Make booking easy** on phones: tap-to-call, online booking and clear directions.
6. **Collect reviews the right way.** Ask in a neutral way, never offer rewards, and never confirm someone is a patient when you reply.
7. **Fix technical basics:** fast pages, secure HTTPS, clear site structure and no broken links.
8. **Update content regularly,** especially when guidelines or treatments change.

## Privacy and tracking tools

Many websites use analytics and advertising pixels. For healthcare providers covered by HIPAA, these tools need care. The US Department of Health and Human Services has published guidance on online tracking technologies. A federal court vacated part of that guidance in June 2024, and HHS notes this at the top of the page, so check the current text with your privacy officer.

Practical steps:

- **List every tag and pixel** on your site and what it sends.
- **Keep tracking off pages** where patients log in, book or share health details, unless your privacy lead approves it.
- **Use a cookie banner** and honor the visitor's choices.

## Choosing an agency

A healthcare SEO agency should understand medical review, privacy and advertising rules, not just rankings. Ask who writes and reviews content, how they handle tracking and reviews, and how they report new patient enquiries. Avoid anyone who promises top rankings or suggests fake reviews.
