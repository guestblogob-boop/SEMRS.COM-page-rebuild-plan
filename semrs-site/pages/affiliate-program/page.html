How the SEMRS affiliate program works

  1. Apply with the form below. We read every application by hand, usually within two business days.
  2. Get your link by email, plus a private dashboard link. Add ?ref=yourcode to any semrs.com address to link straight to that page.
  3. Share it in your posts, videos, newsletters or client notes. A visit sets a 90-day cookie, and the last link clicked wins.
  4. Earn on every order from that visitor, and on their renewals and add-ons during their first year.

Clicks, sales and commissions show in your dashboard as they happen. Nothing to install and nothing to log in to: your private link opens it.

What you earn

Your rate on first orders in the affiliate program rises with your sales in each calendar month:

  • 40% for your 1st to 10th sale of the month.
  • 45% for your 11th to 30th sale.
  • 50% for your 31st to 60th sale.
  • 60% from your 61st sale onward.

Each sale earns the rate of its place in the month, and the count starts again on the 1st. Commissions use the price the customer really pays, after any discount. At today's regular prices:

  • All Pro for 1 website ($145): you earn $58 to $87, plus $17.40 when it renews.
  • All Pro for 5 websites ($299): you earn $119.60 to $179.40, plus $35.88 on renewal.
  • All Pro for unlimited websites ($499): you earn $199.60 to $299.40, plus $59.88 on renewal.
  • Lifetime, unlimited websites ($695 once): you earn $278 to $417.
  • A single add-on such as SEO Pro ($42): you earn $16.80 to $25.20, plus $5.04 on renewal.

See every plan on the SEMRS Pro pricing page. Seasonal sales such as Black Friday bring more buyers, and you still earn your share of each sale.

Rules and payouts

A few simple rules keep the affiliate program fair for partners and customers:

  • Say that you are an affiliate wherever you share your link. The FTC endorsement guides explain how.
  • Do not bid on "SEMRS" or similar brand terms in paid search ads.
  • No spam, no fake reviews, no fake or expired coupon codes, and no misleading claims.
  • Buying through your own link does not earn a commission.
  • Commissions are approved 30 days after the sale, once the refund window has passed. Refunded orders earn nothing.
  • We pay monthly by bank transfer or SadaPay once your approved balance reaches $50.

We can pause an account that breaks these rules. If anything is unclear, ask us first through the contact page.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to join the affiliate program?

No. Joining is free, and you do not need to buy SEMRS Pro first.

How long does the cookie last?

Ninety days. If someone clicks your link and buys within 90 days, the sale is yours, unless they click another partner's link later.

Do I earn on renewals?

Yes. You earn 15% of the renewal and of any add-on that customer buys during their first year. Renewal amounts already include the customer's 20% loyalty discount. Most programs pay nothing after the first order.

How are payouts sent?

By bank transfer, local or international, or by SadaPay. We confirm your details by email before the first payout.

When do I get paid?

Each commission is approved 30 days after the sale. We pay approved commissions monthly, once your balance reaches $50.

Can I share discount codes?

Yes, share the codes we publish, such as seasonal sale codes. Please do not make up codes or list expired ones.
